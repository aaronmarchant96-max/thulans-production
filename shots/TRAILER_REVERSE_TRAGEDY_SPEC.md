# TEASER TRAILER: THE WEIGHT OF A LIE (REVERSE TRAGEDY SPEC)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Creative DNA
* **Project:** The Thulans
* **Format:** 90-Second Cinematic Teaser Trailer
* **Core Concept:** Reverse-Chronology Emotional Tragedy (inspired by *Dead Island* and *Gears of War "Mad World"*)
* **Music Bed:** `assets/audio/The Wall Beneath the Falling.mp3` (Haunting piano, mournful cello, intimate vocal)
* **Governing Hinge:** *"Wulfila volunteered to a lie."*

---

---

### Shot 01: The Dying Titan (`0:00` – `0:22`)
* **Visual:** Extreme slow motion. Single emergency amber strobe piercing thick suspended dust.
* **Camera:** Macro close-up on Varek’s pitted, cracked cast-iron visor. Inside the dark recess, a single weary human eye blinks.
* **Sound Design:** Heavy, raspy respirator breath. Slow, isolated piano notes with distant cavern reverb.
* **Reverse Action:** The massive fractured ceiling slab begins rising *upward* in reverse. Varek's crushed hydraulic leg struts un-buckle, rising back into rigid Anchor State.

---

### Shot 02: The Megalithic Abyss in Reverse (`0:22` – `0:45`)
* **Visual:** Camera pulls backward through the 2,000-meter cavern chasm.
* **Reverse Action:**
  - Fire and sulfur smoke get sucked backward into severed pipes.
  - Thousands of panicking miners run backward into gantry corridors.
  - Blacked-out light towers ignite in reverse sequence, sending god-rays upward through the smog.
* **Voiceover (Varek, radio-filtered, weary):**
  > *“They told us the Mountain was a living god…  
  > They told us she would drink our blood, and give us tomorrow.”*

---

### Shot 03: The High Spires of Garuni (`0:45` – `1:05`)
* **Visual:** Camera ascends through the heavy smog into the gleaming gold-vaulted council chambers.
* **Reverse Action:**
  - Golden valve wheels turn backward.
  - Gushing water from the *Watona* North Aquifer flows backward into iron conduits and is sealed behind heavy deadbolts.
  - Council members in tailored stone robes lock surveyed aquifer maps inside iron vaults.
* **Voiceover (Varek):**
  > *“The water was already there.  
  > The wells were already full.”*

---

### Shot 04: The Choosing at Azg-Haíms (`1:05` – `1:20`)
* **Visual:** Camera drops down the soot shaft into the crowded, impoverished square of the Ash-Hearth.
* **Reverse Action:**
  - A frail, soot-stained teenage boy—**Wulfila**—steps *backward* off the sacrifice platform, out of the iron chains, back into the crowd.
  - His sister, **Haila**, pulls her hands from her face; her tears flow upward back into her eyes.
  - She unties the forged hammer-ring from around his neck and places it back into his trembling hand.

---

### Shot 05: The Hearth & The Stinger (`1:20` – `1:30`)
* **Visual:** Tight, intimate shot inside a humble stone hearth. Wulfila sits by a small warm stove.
* **Action:** A tired, calloused woman’s hand reaches gently into the frame, touching the boy’s warm cheek.
* **Sound:** Music cuts to total silence.
* **Voiceover (Varek, soft, deeply human):**
  > *“Wulfila volunteered to a lie.  
  > ...I had a mother, too.”*
* **Visual Cut:** Smash to Black.
