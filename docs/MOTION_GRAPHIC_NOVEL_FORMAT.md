# Thulans: Narrated Motion Graphic Novel Production Format

## 1. Overview & Strategy

The immediate Thulans production focus shifts away from full 3D character animation toward a **narrated motion graphic novel format**.

This does **not** abandon Blender, CARDO, or the core game concept. It is a lower-cost, high-velocity methodology to establish world lore, writing authenticity, visual identity, and audience loyalty before committing to full 3D production.

---

## 2. Core Format Components

Each episode is an illustrated audio drama featuring:

1. **Aaron's Narration**: Recorded on phone hardware, cleaned and mastered in Reaper (24-bit / 48kHz). Voice remains dominant and dry, avoiding over-processed theatricality.
2. **Original Cinematic Graphic-Novel Illustrations**: High-contrast, industrial, heavily textured 2D plates establishing atmosphere and composition.
3. **Limited Selective Motion**:
   * Multi-plane camera parallax.
   * Drifting soot, particulate, and venting steam.
   * Lantern flame flicker and mechanical shadow shifts.
   * Cable tension vibration and hydraulic recoil.
4. **Selective AI Image-to-Video**: Restrained strictly to controlled 3-to-6-second micro-movements (smoke dissipation, eye shifts, fluid dripping). Never used for complex human kinetics.
5. **Blender Precision Passes**: Mechanically accurate machinery, layered 2.5D projection cards, depth maps, and calculated camera moves.
6. **Suno & Sound Design**: Curated Suno instrumental materials edited, sliced, and mixed in Reaper alongside bespoke industrial foley (lift bells, pneumatic releases, iron clanking).

---

## 3. Episode Scope Comparison

| Metric | Proof-of-Concept Teaser (*Eleven Bells*) | Full Episode (5–8 Minutes) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~56 seconds | 300 – 480 seconds |
| **Narration** | Single continuous locked take | Multi-scene structured monologue/dialogue |
| **Keyframe Illustrations** | 10 – 15 panels | 20 – 25 principal plates |
| **Controlled Motion Clips** | 2 – 4 micro-movements | 6 – 10 controlled clips |
| **Blender 2.5D Passes** | 2 – 3 parallax camera moves | 5 – 8 mechanical/depth passes |
| **Evidence Shots** | 2 aftermath macro plates | 4 – 6 forensic residue plates |
| **Distribution Target** | Vertical/Horizontal social & YouTube teaser | YouTube motion episode + Digital Comic release |

## 4. REI Story Engine: Concept Packet Schema

When tasked with generating Thulan narrative concepts, the REI engine must output structured packets conforming to this exact schema:

```yaml
Packet_ID: THULAN-EP-XXX
Hook: "Opening 5-second mechanical or moral premise"
Human_Subject: "Name, trade, cohort, and physical wear"
Core_Emotional_Wound: "Personal debt, lost kin, or failed oath"
Debt_Pressure: "Council ledger obligations and calorie rations"
Quota_Pressure: "Tonnage, feet bored, or minutes demanded"
Piety_Pressure: "Hearth doctrine vs Council compliance"
Recurring_Image_Or_Phrase: "Motif that shifts meaning across the story"
Physical_Evidence_Token: "Specific tool, scar, fracture, or residue"
Irreversible_Choice: "The exact moment retreat is surrendered"
Reveal: "The mechanical or administrative truth behind the sacrifice"
Final_Complicity_Or_Consequence: "Who profited, who was erased"
Closing_Line: "Final spoken statement anchoring theme"
Canon_Conflicts: "Audit against existing Deephearth lore"
Required_Illustrations:
  - Panel 1: Composition and focal subject
  - Panel 2: Mechanical detail / action
  - Panel 3: Forensic residue / aftermath
```
