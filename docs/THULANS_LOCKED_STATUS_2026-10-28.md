### Candidate visual checks (do not mistake these for locks)

A reader should see clear geography, accurate bread-piece ownership, the actual low concealed hand exchange, distinctive civilian versus patrol footwear, a shut hatch and a **squabbling** coat-sharing beat rather than a sleeping embrace. No invented lanterns or ambient amber flooding. Treat these as defect/acceptance checks for the exploratory opening, **not** automatic author sign-off on generated art. A warm pipe is not an unexplained bright lamp; its exact visual illumination solution is still to be resolved.
