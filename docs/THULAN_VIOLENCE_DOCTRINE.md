# Thulan Violence Doctrine

## 1. The Governing Principle

> **"The violence is fast. Understanding what happened is slow."**

In the Deephearth, human bodies exist in the same physical space as heavy, uncompromising industrial machinery and pneumatic weaponry. Violence is never romantic, choreographic, or drawn-out for spectacle. It does not linger on sadism; it demonstrates consequence.

---

## 2. The Three Beats of Violence

Every violent event in Thulan narrative and animation follows three distinct stages:

```
[1. Impact]       -->   [2. Recognition]   -->   [3. Residue]
Instantaneous            Mechanical               Permanent physical
kinetic trauma           comprehension            evidence & constraint
```

### Beat 1: Impact
* The injury occurs suddenly, often between eye-blinks or within a single mechanical stroke.
* There is no preparatory telegraphing or dramatic slow-motion anticipation. 
* Energy transfers completely and without mercy.

### Beat 2: Recognition
* The audience (and surviving characters) understands exactly what the machinery or weapon did.
* The physical mechanics are plausible, legible, and unyielding.
* The machinery often continues its cycle regardless of the person killed or severed.

### Beat 3: Residue
* Physical evidence remains in the environment and alters subsequent choices.
* Bodies do not despawn. Blood and soot stain stone. Broken gear jams tolerances.
* Survivors must physically navigate the aftermath to continue.

## 3. Canonical Voice & Tonal Touchstones

* *"The shield stopped the hammer. His ribs did not."*
* *"The lift took his arm at the elbow. It reached the next tier on schedule."*
* *"The piston fired before his hand cleared the collar. His gauntlet remained closed. Everything inside it became flat."*
* *"The machinery never noticed the flesh. It simply finished the stroke."*

---

## 4. Strict Constraints & Prohibitions

1. **No Torture Spectacle**: Violence is industrial, operational, or desperate—never gratuitous torture porn.
2. **No Endless Screaming**: High trauma in high pressure causes shock, wind-loss, and silence. Screams are cut short by physics.
3. **No Action-Movie Quips**: Characters do not deliver one-liners before or after taking a life. Breathing and checking seals take precedence.
4. **No Physically Impossible Survival**: Armor protects against fragmentation and glancing blows; it does not nullify hydraulic crushing or direct kinetic shockwaves.

---

## 5. Thulan Warrior Identity & The Metric of Honor

The martial ethic of the Thulans is rooted in Varek’s maxim:

> **"Strength is measured by what you stand between."**

* **The Measure of Worth**: Thulan warriors take pride in holding elevator throats, bridges, pressure doors, aquifers, ventilation trunks, and civilian retreat routes.
* **Accounting**: Honor is not tallied in enemy body counts, but in **minutes held** and **civilians passed to safety**.
* **Council Corruption**: The tragic arc of the Thulan warrior caste is the Council's steady weaponization of this courage:
  $$\text{“Hold for your Hearth”} \longrightarrow \text{“Die where Provision assigns you.”}$$
