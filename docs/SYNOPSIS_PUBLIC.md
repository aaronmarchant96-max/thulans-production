# THE THULANS
*A post-human rescue saga about who gets to decide what another human being must endure.*

**Synopsis:**

Centuries after the civilization that made him has vanished, an ancient rescue giant
awakens inside a sacred mountain city dying from within. As he searches for traces of
the people he lost, he discovers that the virtues he remembers — endurance, sacrifice
and duty — have become instruments of control, forcing him to decide how much truth a
civilization built on lies can survive.
