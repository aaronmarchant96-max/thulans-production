# THE THULANS
*An industrial tragedy about who gets to decide what another person must endure.*

**Synopsis:**

Three hundred years ago, a rescuer named Varek was sealed inside an iron machine so he
could hold up what would otherwise fall on people. He wakes into the same mountain city
he served — and a stranger one.

His people survived. Their world did not. The virtues he remembers — endurance, sacrifice,
duty — have been turned into instruments of control. The rulers of the dying mountain have
learned to fake miracles. The strongest people in it are being persuaded to die for
miracles that never happened — and the ones who volunteer are the ones who believed
hardest.

As Varek searches for what is left of the home he knew, he uncovers a crime the whole city
depends on keeping hidden — and is forced to decide how much truth a civilization built on
lies can survive.
