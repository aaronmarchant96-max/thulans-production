The 21 October repository snapshot contains **22,577 whitespace-separated words** of chapter and act text, including headings and excluding cover/front matter. This is not a fresh word count of the later live draft. The former ~40,000-word target is historical. The reductions in Chapters Seven–Ten are intentional revisions.

Scarcity is real; the claim that a death produces resources is fraudulent. Varek brings proof expecting correction, receives confirmation, and returns to help the people below. The novella ends with a struggle over care, ownership, and memory; it does not dramatize a mountain-wide collapse caused by publication of the evidence.

## Characters and chronology

- Bram is thirteen during the main action and turns fourteen on 30 October.
- Sanna is eighteen; Wulfila was nineteen. They are siblings. Haila was Wulfila’s betrothed.
- Kaelen is the drift smith; his grandfather Edrik cast the bearing plate found inside Varek’s hip.
- Varek has served for three centuries. He attended Wulfila’s surrender forty days before the main action and was then returned to Caisson Seven.
- The survey and Wulfila’s own sounding are dated 10 September. He discusses the plan on 11 September and dies on 12 September. The present action is 22 October.
- Tap Twelve ran two days after the surrender and was buried on the fourth day. Bram was drafted on the sixth day.
- The final domestic scene takes place six years after Varek’s death.

## Four acts and twelve chapters

### ACT I: THE SUMP & THE SUNDAY LIE

**CHAPTER ONE: The Flange & The Pine Sprig**

Bram and Kaelen salvage rail bolts in Drift 14. Bram finds Wulfila’s hidden crew chit, shows Kaelen after the track-walkers pass, and conceals it again. Warm rock reveals running water beyond the wall.

**CHAPTER TWO: The Nursery in Lower Four**

Sanna takes responsibility for an unauthorized pump repair after Odo reduces the household allowance from eight pints to six. Shared broth and Bram’s resistance to her tending his thumb establish the siblings’ care and friction.

**CHAPTER THREE: The Memory of the Leap**

Bram and Kaelen enter the locker house during rail clearance. A flashback establishes Wulfila’s reasons for accepting the witnessed exemption despite knowing water exists. His surrender occurs on 12 September. Haila’s scorched-scarf memory recalls the man outside the ceremony. The recovered slate records the 10 September sounding.

### ACT II: THE TWO LEDGERS

**CHAPTER FOUR: The Acoustic Trace**

Sanna first wants to destroy the dangerous evidence, then chooses to open a small draw-off valve. A seismic rupture releases Varek from his cradle and injures his lower back. He recalls trusting the recorded eight-pint allowance. His breach breaks the sill, sending dirty water toward Lower Four; he catches the falling roof above Sanna.

**CHAPTER FIVE: The Shoring of Drift 14**

The crew shores the roof and Varek closes the ruptured inspection pipe. The slate, working shipyard pumps, plate deliveries, and berth totals establish the withheld water and exclusion from evacuation. They open the clean draw-off into separate gutters. Bram drinks before the alarm brings enforcement.

**CHAPTER SIX: The Welded Advance**

Varek disables two Welded mining rigs rather than killing their trapped operators. Bram breaks the salvage foreman’s arm before a match reaches the charge fuse. Varek kills the guard who aims at Bram. Further damage ends his ability to replenish walking pressure; the ram deadline begins.

### ACT III: THE ARITHMETIC OF THE SPIRE

**CHAPTER SEVEN: The Ascent**

Sanna trades the nursery boiler’s remaining steam for Varek’s loading pressure while keeping the clean feeder open and organizing bailers. Kaelen recognizes Edrik’s bearing mark; Bram supplies a replacement cover bolt. The descending forty-tonne stone cage lifts Varek. Tomas provides an upper service charge. A running washroom tap precedes the office.

**CHAPTER EIGHT: The Office on Tier Nine**

Varek demands recall of the rams and continued water. Theodemir refuses, verifies the sounding, and acknowledges the choices behind the allocations. The ledger already reads NIL; he adds Wulfila’s name without altering it. Varek confronts what he knew about the berth total. Theodemir signs the survey confirmation and feeder-closure order. Varek disconnects the service hose himself and leaves with the evidence.

**CHAPTER NINE: The Hammer Falls**

Varek retains the strength to kill Theodemir but learns the entered order would survive through Deputy Gero. He drops the maul and gives the frightened clerk room. Tomas helps him into the gravity cage; the emptied opposing cage permits descent. Workers bring him by trolley to Lower Four. The rams remain mobilized.

### ACT IV: THE DISMANTLING

**CHAPTER TEN: Bring the Wrenches**

Sanna obtains Varek’s consent and accepts Bram’s insistence on staying. The crew isolates the rescue pump from the breathing engine, removes it, and converts it to hand operation. The water level holds. Varek’s old injury becomes visible; they prepare the inner cradle and test bellows before lifting him.

**CHAPTER ELEVEN: Skin Against Skin**

Sanna touches the helmet, then the man’s hand. The crew lifts his injured body clear while preserving air support, then carries him through the wash passage on hand bellows. His foundry childhood returns in memory. He dies holding Sanna’s hand as ram blows reach the passage.

**CHAPTER TWELVE: Our Iron Anyway**

The line crew closes the draw-off and charges collapse the haulage throat. Bram has Tomas read and copy the signed evidence; he later corrects false embellishments. Malakor records no recoverable nursery assets while recovering the empty frame separately. Six years later the state claims Varek through a newly cast bronze monument and a separately refitted empty iron shell. At the dedication, a foundry worker asks Bram for a copy of the order for his shift; Bram invites him to Lower Four after the bell to read it together. The workshop, ticket/rod obstruction, shared bread, working pump, and Sanna’s final answer remain. This approved small act of organization opens toward the planned rebellion.

## Continuity that controls revision

- The Fault Maul is 30 kg total, including its head and shaft.
- Varek’s walking boiler drives oil hydraulics. The smaller breathing engine has an independent pilot burner. Running the rescue pump from that engine would take capacity needed for his air; the removed water pump is operated by hand.
- The lower-back injury occurs in the Chapter Four fall. Removing the rescue pump does not cause his fatal injury.
- The damaged walking leg is the right. The split collarbone gauge branch is isolated before the ascent; its zero reading is no longer a useful pressure measure.
- Clean water runs in gutters separate from the dirty ditch and waste channel. After the throat is charged, rubble seepage is not treated as drinking water.
- The clerk’s existing ledger entry is SURRENDER VALUE: NIL. The signed sheet carried out by Varek confirms the sounding and closure order; it does not contain that ledger entry.
- Varek disables the Welded, kills the guard only to prevent Bram being shot, and refuses revenge in the office corridor.
- Varek’s death is final. The later workshop frame is an empty mechanism, not a revived or surviving consciousness.
- ORA ET LABORA is on an inner plate behind the chest guard; FORTIS ET LIBER is on the maul collar. Latin survives as archaic stamped language.
- Bram’s apron belongs to Sanna. The slate was hidden by Wulfila and retrieved in Chapter Three; it was not purchased from a clerk.
- Preserve the approved cover and existing chapter plates. The live Doc was checked with twelve inline images on 24 October; Chapter Eleven intentionally has no chapter plate. This documentation update does not synchronize image assets. Reading manuscripts contain fiction, not appended production notes.
