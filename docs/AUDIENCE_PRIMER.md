# THE THULANS — Audience Primer
*The one page a first-time viewer needs. Everything else is discovered.*

---

## The hook

A man sealed inside an iron rescue suit three hundred years ago wakes into a mountain
city that is running out of everything. Its rulers have learned to fake miracles: hide a
water or ore find, let the people suffer, then accept a volunteer's life and "reveal" the
resource as an answer to blood. When the man proves the miracles were staged, the truth
does not free anyone. It brings the whole world down.

---

## Five things to know

1. **The whole world is inside a mountain.** There is no sky and no outside. Three
   thousand years of civilization, stacked in layers — clean and rich at the top, working
   poor at the bottom.
2. **It is dying.** Four point two million people are left in tunnels built for forty
   million. The ore is nearly gone and the rock is starting to fail.
3. **The rulers know.** A Council of three hides how little remains, and builds six escape
   ships that fit one person in seven.
4. **The faith is manufactured.** The "miracles" that restore water and ore were found by
   surveyors *before* the sacrifices that supposedly earned them.
5. **The hero cannot fix it.** Varek is a rescuer, not a revolutionary. He tells the
   truth, and the truth destroys the one thing holding the city together: the lie.

---

## Who to watch (six names)

- **Varek** — the man in the iron suit. Three hundred years old, sealed inside a walking
  rescue machine. Believes duty means standing between people and what would crush them.
- **Wulfila** — a nineteen-year-old who volunteers to die, honestly believing it will save
  his family's water. He was lied to.
- **Sanna** — Wulfila's sister. Runs the nursery, keeps the survivors alive. The story ends
  in her hands.
- **Bram** — Wulfila's fourteen-year-old brother. Drafted into the mines anyway.
- **Haila** — Wulfila's betrothed. She tied the ring that marked him for the sacrifice.
- **Theodemir** — the Council's numbers man. Not a monster. Just certain that the
  arithmetic matters more than the truth.

---

## The one rule

**The state manufactures miracles to make honest people volunteer to die.**

Teach this once. Everything else — the marrow market, the water entitlements, the escape
ships — is simply how the mountain got there.

## What we do not explain

The deep names — *Fairguna*, *Azg-Haíms*, *the Wahlja*, *Faírg-Tygil*, *blôþ-stáin* — stay
in the world as texture. They are never introduced on first contact and no scene depends
on the audience understanding them. See `docs/AUDIENCE_NAMES.md` for the full gate.
