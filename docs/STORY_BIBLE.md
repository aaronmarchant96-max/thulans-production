# THE THULANS: CINEMATIC STORY BIBLE (TRACK `CINEMATIC-001` / RECONCILED)

---

## 1. The Canonical Spine

### A. The Master Truth (`THE_DISMANTLING_OF_VAREK.md:31-49`)
The Mountain is dying. Magma displacement in the lower mantle gives the city between eleven and twenty-eight years before structural collapse. The Council is secretly constructing six generational evacuation hulls on the slips—capable of carrying only 600,000 of the city's 4.2 million people. On those arks, the ration forecast is **"two pints of gray water a week"** (`DISMANTLING:49`).

Scarcity is terrifyingly real. The state's crime is not inventing the drought, but **withholding confirmed reserves** as a political lever to force exhausting labor:
> *"Forty days before Wulfila took the leap off the gallows platform... The north cistern had eleven million gallons in reserve. We had the sounding on the third of the month."* (`DISMANTLING:31`)
> *"He jumped so forty thousand men would keep cutting timber... If Azg-Haíms knew there was water, they’d have struck for twelve-hour shifts instead of sixteen."* (`DISMANTLING:41`)

### B. The Cinematography Doctrine: RULING 001 with the Awe Exception
- **Default Framing (Human Scale)**: All scenes centered on human agents remain at intimate, tactile scale. The Mountain is experienced through the individuals it crushes.
- **The Awe Exception**: Establishing and transitional shots are permitted to render the Mountain at colossal scale (cavernous brass-and-iron halls, Great Central Rift, tiered industrial verticality) to convey institutional power and insignificance. The juxtaposition between monumental scale and the individual soul is the core narrative engine.

### C. The Canon Boundary (Local vs. Global) & Alternate Timelines
- **The Global Line (Varek's Path)**: The secret evacuation hulls, the Archon's secret evacuation arithmetic, and the public dismantling of Varek belong strictly to the master storyline (`DISMANTLING:31-49`). The Steward's local discoveries never preempt Varek's public revelation.
- **Interactive Framing Device**: The Hearth Steward at [Day +40] in Records Cell 4 is an interactive perspective frame, positioned contemporaneously with the Shaft 44 crisis (`VAREK_ACTS:82`).
- **Alternate Timeline Authority (Ruling: Aaron Marchant, 2026-09-17)**:
  - **Prime Canon Timeline (`BURY`)**: Follows the locked screenplay canon (`FEATURE_PITCH:52`, `RULINGS:712`). The lie holds; 16-hour timber shifts continue; Bram enters Shaft 44 on schedule, where the canon roof shear occurs and crushes his leg.
  - **Authorized Alternate Timelines (`EXPOSE` / `LEVERAGE`)**: The player's administrative choices can diverge from screenplay canon to pull Bram off the shift:
    - *EXPOSE*: Nailing the sounding date triggers the Azg-Haíms timber strike for 12-hour shifts (`DISMANTLING:41`). Timber halts; miners walk out; Bram is pulled off the shift in protest before the cave-in occurs, at the cost of immediate Council reprisals.
    - *LEVERAGE*: The Steward sells advance sounding intelligence to House Halza (`RULING 008`). The grain payment bribes the assignment clerk to pull Bram from the roster, sparing him at the cost of enabling Halza's profiteering and local water hoarding.
