### Page 1 - The record / the warm pipe

**1.** Black. Small, plain lettering, copied from an equipment report:

**FRAME 9. NO FAULT FOUND.**

**2.** Beneath a warm return pipe, behind two loose boards, Ansen watches the passage through a gap. Nessa sits behind him, her skirt tucked beneath her knees to keep it out of the floor water. His oversized coat bunches around his ankles.

Through the gap: workers' plain clogs and cloth-wrapped legs, the bottom of a service hatch, and a strip of light.

**3.** Nessa draws a small loaf from beneath her blouse. Ansen turns immediately.

She points him back toward the gap.

**NESSA:** Watch.

### Page 2 - The bread game

**1.** Nessa breaks the loaf against her knee. Crumbs fall into her skirt. She gives Ansen the smaller piece.

Through the gap, a neighbouring worker waits at the tin-warming hatch, hand held out.

**NESSA:** Half each.

**WORKER (off):** And my tin?

**2.** Hobnailed patrol boots pass close outside.

Both siblings freeze, their faces and shoulders still, eyes on the gap. Below the boards, Ansen's hands exchange their pieces of bread.

**3.** The patrol boots have gone.

Nessa looks at the piece now in her hand. Ansen meets her eyes, chewing nothing.

**NESSA:** That was mine.

**ANSEN:** You said half each.

**4.** Nessa has taken an enormous bite from her smaller piece. The heat catches her. Her eyes water, but she stubbornly holds Ansen's stare.

Ansen is laughing.

**5.** The hatch has shut. Its strip of light is gone.

Outside, meal tins rattle together as the worker moves off. Ansen holds up the side of his oversized coat. Nessa squeezes beneath it, her hip pushing him against the boards.

**ANSEN:** Shift over.

**NESSA:** You called me over.

### Page 3 - Their place

**1.** A drop falls from the pipe into Nessa's collar. Her cold hand is already against Ansen's ribs beneath the coat.

He flinches. She looks pleased with herself.

**2.** Their shoulders have settled together. Ansen's hand rests over Nessa's cold hand, keeping it against his ribs.

She hums three notes and a wandering fourth. He offers a different fourth. She gives him a sideways look.

**3.** Ansen winds a loose thread from Nessa's cuff around his finger.

She has noticed.

**NESSA:** Do not pull that.

**4.** A little of the seam has opened.

Ansen presses it flat with his thumb, attempting to conceal the damage. Nessa watches him. Her mouth starts to give.

Neither is watching the gap.

### Page 4 - Three months

**1.** The boards wrench outward. Light strikes their faces. A stores guard reaches into the shelter.

**2.** The guard grips Nessa's wrist. Ansen catches her sleeve.

The cuff tears along the loosened seam.

**3.** At intake, the remains of the same loaf lie beside two entries. A clerk writes:

**UPPER SPIRE STORES - THEFT. THREE MONTHS.**

Ansen still holds the strip of cuff.

**4.** A fitter opens Ansen's hand. The torn cloth drops into a tray of belongings.

His oversized coat lies in the tray.

Beyond him, a number plate is attached to Nessa's frame:

**9**

**5.** Cuffs close around Nessa's arms. Ansen tries to keep her in sight as a screen slides between them.

A feeding tube touches his mouth.

**NESSA (behind the screen):** Ansen?

## Candidate decisions and pending work

The sequence for this candidate is: hide, split, freeze, swap, retaliate, share warmth, settle, damage the cuff, arrest. Nessa burns her mouth on Page 2; the superseded Ansen hot-bite and repeated bread-contest notes do not apply to this candidate. The siblings remain behind the boards throughout the shelter sequence. The hatch warms meal tins and supplies no second loaf.
