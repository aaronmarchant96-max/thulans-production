## 1. The Core Identity & World Pitch

> **“Brothar Varek Fairgunjis, Thulan of the Old Hearths.”**

* **The Full Ceremonial Title:** **Brothar Varek Fairgunjis, Thulan of the Old Hearths**
  * **Brothar** (*Gothic: Broþar*): Not an ecclesiastical title, but the solemn brotherhood of the *Giba-Broþrans* and shared mortal kinship (*"We get each other home"*).
  * **Fairgunjis** (*Gothic Genitive*): “Of the Mountain” / “From the Mountain.”
  * **Thulan of the Old Hearths:** Rooted in the ancient, uncorrupted era when protection and rescue were sacred duties, long before the modern regime's industrial exploitation.
* **The Thematic Irony:** He carries the Mountain in his name, his culture, and his ancestral duty—only to discover centuries later that the Mountain was never the whole world, and that its masters planned to abandon him and his people within its dying shell.
* **The Setting:** A civilization surviving across and beneath a continent-sized **Megalithic Mountain** (*Fairguni*).
* **The Governing Tragedy:** The Thulans were not created by modern exploiters; they were engineered thousands of years ago when protection was still sacred. In the dying modern era, their symbols, mottoes, and memories have been twisted into **state industrial propaganda** to force exhausted miners to dig while the mountain collapses.
* **The Protagonist’s Weight:** Varek is not a revolutionary demagogue. He is politically explosive simply because **he remembers the truth**. When the state claims the Thulans died for production quotas, Varek is the living witness who says: **“No.”**

## 2. The Supreme Creative Guardrail: The Intimacy Rule

> ### **THE INTIMACY RULE**
> **No faction, technology, religion, conflict, or historical event should exist only to make the setting larger. Every major element must connect to the lived cost of the Mountain’s vertical hierarchy, the people trapped within it, or Varek’s memory of those he once endured beside.**
>
> **The world may be enormous. The story must remain personal.**
>
> **“Never let the Mountain become bigger than the people crushed beneath it.”**

### The 7 Diagnostic Questions for All Future Lore
Before any new element is made canon, it must answer these seven questions:
1. **Who has to climb farther because of this?**
2. **Who works deeper because of this?**
3. **Who is left behind because of this?**
4. **Who benefits from it?**
5. **Who pays for it?**
6. **What would Varek remember when he sees it?**
7. **How would one of his *Giba-Broþrans* have experienced it?

### A. The Mountain as the Universe
To almost everyone alive, **the Mountain is the world**. Generational isolation has made the idea of an exterior horizon or open sky seem physically absurd:
* A person can travel upwards for weeks through bustling caverns, residential wards, and transit tunnels and remain entirely inside stone.
* Distance is measured in levels and elevator drops: **Up-Mountain**, **Down-Mountain**, **Coreward**, and **Rimward**.
  > **The poor dig downward.**  
  > **The powerful climb upward.**

## 4. Foundation Question 1: Faith, Manufactured Miracles & The Life Tithe

> **“The Mountain never demanded sacrifice. People taught other people to sacrifice themselves to it.”**  
> **“The oldest faith taught stewardship. The dying civilization converted stewardship into extraction.”**  
> **“Ancient faith: the strong sacrifice FOR the vulnerable. Modern faith: the vulnerable are sacrificed FOR the system.”**

```

                              │

  • "The Mother provides for those who honor her."
                              │

  • "Labor honors the Mother."

  • "The Mother withholds because we have become unworthy."
                              │
[ STAGE 5: MANUFACTURED CAUSALITY & THE REVEAL RITE ]
  • "ENDURE THE MOUNTAIN." (Whatever the stone inflicts, you must bear silently).
  • "Only blood and sacrifice can restore her favor. Ore requires life."
```

### A. The Semantic Corruption: "Endure FOR" vs "Endure THE"
* **Ancient:** **“ENDURE FOR THE MOUNTAIN.”** (Hardship has purpose and limits; the strong bear the burden so the vulnerable survive.)
* **Modern:** **“ENDURE THE MOUNTAIN.”** (Suffering itself is made proof of faith; whatever horrors the dying mountain inflicts, the weak must suffer silently.)

### B. Manufactured Divine Causality (The Reveal Rite)
The summit does not simply preach faith; **they actively engineer the evidence to prove the faith is true**:
1. Geological surveyors discover a rich titanium vein or clean water cavern. Telemetry is classified.
2. The Summit commands the priesthood to declare that the Mother is "withholding" due to lack of devotion.
3. Desperate local councils conduct public penance, quotas, or a **Life Tithe** (sacrificing souls down a shaft).
4. Days later, the Summit "reveals" the new vein and ships supplies: *“The Mother has answered our blood!”*
5. **The Intimate Tragedy:** A mother watches her son cast down a shaft during drought; three days later, water arrives. She spends her life believing *his death saved the city*, unaware the water shipment was approved a month before.

### C. Varek's Lethal Challenges
* **On Blood Sacrifice:**
  > **Priest:** *“The Mother demands a life so that thousands may endure! How could you know?”*  
  > **Varek:** **“Because we were the ones who taught you her name.”**
* **On the Staged Miracles:**
  > *(Priesthood proclaims a newly opened vein after a blood rite)*  
  > **Varek:** **“When was it surveyed?”**

## 5. Mountain Warfare Mechanics

Because space is vertical, military conflict in the Mountain follows unique industrial rules:

1. **Ventilation Sieges:** Armies seize upper air-intake shafts and pump sulfur/carbon monoxide down-mountain to suffocate rival cities.
2. **Aquifer Diversions:** Dynamiting subterranean reservoir gates to cut off water supply and force unconditional surrender.
3. **Foundation Undermining:** Excavating beneath a rival city's load-bearing basalt arches until their entire district slides into the abyss.
4. **Elevator Bastions:** Heavily fortified transit choke-points equipped with counterweight guillotine drop-gates controlling movement between levels.

## 7. The Climax & The Void's Semantic Leverage

When the hollow mountain finally suffers cascading structural collapse:
1. **The Archons Launch:** Elite atmospheric arks blast off through the cloud barrier toward the outer continents.
2. **The Mountain Fails:** Billions of tons of basalt buckle inward; the false god crumbles.
3. **The Thulan Stands:** In the deep sectors, Varek steps between the falling ceiling and the fleeing families, locking his external structural frame into *Anchor State*.
4. **The Void's Precision Strike:**
   * The corrupted state told millions: *“Endure because you must.”*
   * The Void tells Varek: *“Endure because someone else will die if you don't.”*
   * The Void traps Varek by speaking his **pure, ancient truth**, not the state's corrupted lie:
     > *“You would endure anything to save them. So endure me.”*
