### Subsystem Breakdown:

1. **The Overhead Tectonic Yoke:**
   - Manganese-forged roll arch anchored to the dorsal load rails.
   - Curved forward over the pilot's helm to catch, split, and deflect falling ceiling slabs.
   - Integrated with four heavy forged rigging eyes for deep-shaft crane extraction.

2. **The Thoracic Structural Load Cage & Protective Life-Support Hull:**
   - External open roll-cage of structural box-girders bolted around an inner, hermetically welded titanium-lead biological sarcophagus.
   - Chest station incorporates analog oil-pressure gauges, pneumatic reservoir dials, and dual heavy-duty rotary dust/gas filtration canisters.
   - 100% industrial brutalism: visible Grade-8 flange bolts, grease ports, inspection plates, and hydraulic line routing.

3. **Integrated Diesel-Hydraulic Powerplant Chassis:**
   - **The engine is the spine.** The opposed-piston diesel-hydraulic block forms the load-bearing center section connecting the dorsal rails to the pelvic cradle.
   - Thermal dissipation louvers set into reinforced side cowlings.
   - High-pressure hydraulic accumulator canisters recessed into armored spine pockets.
   - Twin soot-baffled exhaust chimneys angled backward behind the yoke with integrated spark arrestors.

4. **Asymmetric Load Stations:**
   - **Left Shoulder — The *Gren-Skildus* (Ancestral Load Plate):**
     - Hand-hammered from native green copper-bearing iron ore by his mortal family's smiths in *Azg-Haíms*.
     - Broad, convex deflection plate with stamped Thulan cohort runic tallies.
     - Mounted to a heavy double-pin articulating pivot to allow active shoring bracing.
   - **Right Shoulder — Industrial Rigging Crane Hitch:**
     - Exposed heavy hydraulic pivot, hoist hook receiver, and universal hydraulic power-takeoff dock for rescue tools.

5. **Pelvis, Legs & Knee Lockouts:**
   - Heavy cast-iron pelvic ring with dual-clevis femoral pivots.
   - Femurs reinforced with external hydraulic assist pistons.
   - Knees equipped with **Rotary Positive-Lock Valves**: when engaged during cave-in events, hydraulic fluid is trapped in the cylinders, converting the articulated leg into a rigid structural column.

6. **Triple-Mode Ground Interface:**
   - **Bedrock / Solid Stone:** Pneumatically fired tungsten-carbide heel pins driven into the rock stratum.
   - **Structural Iron Decking / Grate:** High-flux electro-magnetic sole plates and mechanical flange-clamping cleats.
   - **Unstable Debris / Loose Shingle:** Splayed outrigger pads with hardened multi-tooth friction cleats for wide weight distribution.

* **Type:** Two-Handed Variable-Output Pneumatic Kinetic Impactor & Shoring Tool.
* **Function:** Used to shatter seismic rock jams, drive structural shoring wedges, or open structural rescue channels.
* **Design:**
  - Solid hexagonal cast-steel shaft with knurled grip sleeves.
  - Head contains an internal pneumatic piston charged from the rig’s air accumulator.
  - Variable kinetic output tailored to rock density to manage recoil into the frame.
  - Reverse face features a heavy prying pick for levering collapsed granite slabs.

### B. The Thermal Vein Cutter (*Haíti-Skreid*)
* **Type:** Guarded Electric Arc-Plasma Thermal Shear.
* **Function:** Rapidly cuts through twisted structural I-beams, collapsed rails, and reinforcing rod during disaster extraction.
* **Design:**
  - High-intensity electric arc-plasma cutting torch powered directly by the rig's internal generator bus (eliminating dangerous combustible gas tanks in collapsing mine shafts).
  - Heavy armored power cable with flexible steel overbraid routed to the right tool-dock manifold.
  - Heavy ceramic-insulated slag guard to protect the operator during close-quarters structural cuts.
