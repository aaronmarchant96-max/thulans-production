## 4. Substrate-Specific Anchor Assembly

The anchor assembly integrates three mechanical locking modes:

1. **Drill / Pin Mode (Bedrock & Solid Granite):**
   - Central heavy conical tungsten-carbide pin driven directly into rock strata by single-shot pneumatic/hydraulic percussion.
2. **Mag Clamp Mode (Structural Iron Decking & Grates):**
   - High-flux magnetic contact sole plates paired with perimeter gripping jaws that seize deck flanges.
3. **Outrigger Mode (Unstable Rubble & Broken Shingle):**
   - Splayed articulated stabilizer outriggers extending laterally with hardened multi-tooth cleats to maximize contact surface and prevent sinkage.
