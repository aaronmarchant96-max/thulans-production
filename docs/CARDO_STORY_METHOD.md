# CARDO REI NARRATIVE METHODOLOGY & THE HINGE SPEC
*Authority: Aaron Marchant | Framework: PromptHound Labs / CARDO REI*

---

## 1. What is the CARDO Method in Story Writing?
*Cardo* is Latin for "the hinge of the matter"—the load-bearing pivot point where the trajectory of the scene, character, or conflict irreversibly changes.

In fiction, CARDO enforces that:
1. **Every Scene Must Have an Identifiable Hinge**:
   - The scene cannot just be descriptive drift or dialogue padding.
   - You must identify the exact pivot where the structural assumption breaks or the character's internal want/fear collides with an immovable physical or political reality.
2. **Phase 0 — The Character Driver Hinge**:
   - What does the protagonist specifically **want** in this scene?
   - What do they **fear**?
   - What false structural assumption are they operating on?
   - What physical event snaps that assumption?
3. **The 3 Invariants of the Hinge**:
   - **Show, Do Not Summarize**: Never state that a character is afraid, conflicted, or trembling. Reveal psychological stakes entirely through involuntary physical actions, sharp subtextual dialogue, breathing, and precise behavioral choices.
   - **Never Announce the Twist / Hinge**: Never explain the pivot to the reader. Resolve it strictly through tangible physical events, character choices, and irreversible consequences.
   - **Conclusion Discipline**: Do not conclude by summarizing or moralizing what the protagonist lost, learned, or became. End with a concrete physical action, image, or line of dialogue that allows the reader to infer the consequence.

## 2. The 5 CARDO Questions for Every Chapter Beat
| Question | What It Prevents |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. What is the physical constraint of this space?** | Floating heads, zero-gravity dialogue, generic rooms. |
| **2. What is the character's immediate material need?** | Abstract philosophical posturing. |
| **3. Where is the Hinge (the pivot point)?** | Aimless filler scenes that don't advance causal stakes. |
| **4. What concrete physical cost is paid?** | Consequence-free action and cheap victories. |
| **5. Does this survive the Banned Slop audit?** | AI clichés, puffed-up metaphors, and melodrama. |
