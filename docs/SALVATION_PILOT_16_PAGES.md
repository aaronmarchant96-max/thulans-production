# The Thulans: Salvation
### Sixteen-page graphic-novel pilot — revision 2

**Date:** 27 October 2026

**Status:** Working script responding to the author's page-count, voice, record-payoff, and continuity notes. Exactly 16 story pages, 66 panels; cover and credits are outside this count. Animation runtime is not inferred from page count.

**Names:** Ansi and Nasa remain the approved working names pending the author's response to the ANSI/NASA collision. No replacement is silently adopted.

**Continuity:** Varek's rescue and the freed miner's later presence derive from the author-supplied novella export. The side gallery, Frame 9 designation, individual handler, controls, and attempted escape are **Salvation-specific pilot staging**, not scenes already present in the novella or an alternate history replacing it. The wider siblings/sentencing premise and tragic outcome are approved in [RULING 035](../RULINGS.md#ruling-035-welded). Final integration needs a route and timing check after Ansi's Chapter Ten/Eleven presence. Nasa is not the second miner Varek frees.

**Voice:** The export's rescue line is “It's stopped. I have you.” This adaptation uses the author's requested “It has stopped. I have you.” No contraction is used in Varek's new dialogue; the manuscript itself is unchanged.

**Form:** Mostly forward. Page 16 alone returns to an earlier action. Pages 11–12 use the same four-panel grid and matched compositions. The first releases a restraint; the second fails to release a load-bearing coupling. Final mechanical geometry remains to be blocked, not treated as verified engineering.

**Sound, if animated:** An original restrained work-song, introduced through the siblings, continues gently through the fatal action. No borrowed recording, fixed pitches, or exact lyric-to-frame cues are approved. Quiet machinery and forced breath remain audible. On the page, restrained lettering and repeated compositions carry the rhythm.

## Page 1 — The record / the warm pipe

**1.** Black. Small, plain lettering, as copied from an equipment report:

**FRAME 9. NO FAULT FOUND.**

**2.** Beneath a warm return pipe, behind two loose boards, Ansi watches a passage through a gap. Nasa sits behind him. They are young teenagers; exact ages are not assigned by this script. His coat is too large. Her skirt is tucked beneath her knees to keep it out of the floor water.

**3.** Nasa draws a small loaf from beneath her blouse. Ansi turns immediately. She points him back toward the gap.

> **NASA:** Watch.

## Page 2 — The bread

**1.** Ansi faces the gap. His hand reaches backward. Nasa moves the loaf out of reach without looking at it.

> **NASA:** You stood in the door.
>
> **ANSI:** You told me to watch it.

**2.** She holds the loaf between them.

> **NASA:** From beside it.

**3.** Ansi braces the warm loaf against his coat and breaks it. The crust cracks. Both look toward the boards.

**4.** Boots pass outside. They hold the pieces motionless until the footsteps recede.

**5.** Nasa takes the larger piece and bites. Ansi looks at his portion, then hers. She tears off a tiny scrap and puts it on his. He waits. She loses the straight face and gives him a proper piece.

## Page 3 — Their place

**1.** Ansi bites too soon. Heat catches his mouth. Nasa watches him breathe through his teeth.

> **NASA:** Every time.

**2.** He waits for her to make the same mistake. She blows across her piece, deliberately, then eats. Her shoulder nudges his. A few crumbs lie between them.

**3.** Water drops inside her collar. He raises his elbow. She slips her cold hand beneath his coat. He flinches; when she starts to withdraw it, he puts it back against his ribs. She hums three notes and a wandering fourth. He supplies another fourth. She repeats hers more firmly.

**4.** They have settled together. Ansi winds a loose thread from her cuff around a finger.

> **NASA:** Do not pull that.

A little of the seam has already opened. He presses it flat with his thumb. She sees what he is doing and starts to laugh. Neither is watching the boards.

## Page 4 — Three months

**1.** The boards wrench outward. Light strikes their faces. A stores guard reaches into their shelter.

**2.** The guard grips Nasa's wrist. Ansi catches her sleeve. The cuff tears along the loosened seam.

**3.** At intake, their bread lies beside two entries. A clerk writes **UPPER SPIRE STORES — THEFT. THREE MONTHS.** Ansi still holds the strip of cuff.

**4.** A fitter opens his hand. The cloth drops into a tray with their belongings. Beyond him, a number plate is attached to Nasa's frame: **9**.

**5.** Cuffs close around her arms. Ansi tries to keep her in sight as a screen slides between them. A feeding tube touches his mouth.

> **NASA, behind the screen:** Ansi?

## Page 5 — The same rivet

**1.** A narrow mask slit. Beyond it, a breaker rises above a strip of stone. One clean rivet is visible at the edge of the opening.

**2.** The same composition. The rivet has darkened. His larger hand fills more of its cuff. A new leather strap lies over an old sore.

**3.** The same composition. Rust has spread around the rivet. The breaker catches against a sleeper. Its drive keeps turning.

**4.** His hand hangs below the cuff at a wrong angle. The next stroke starts.

> **ANSI:** Can't stop it.

## Page 6 — I have you

**1.** Slit view: Kaelen's lamp and worn apron beside the track.

> **ANSI:** Please. My wrist is broke.

**2.** Varek enters the opening. The maul passes Ansi's face toward the drive pin.

**3.** **CLANG.** The pin breaks. The breaker falls out of view.

**4.** Iron talons catch the frame. Varek closes its steam supply and vents upward. Ansi's injured hand is no longer being pulled through another stroke.

**5.** Hold the slit composition after the expected movement has failed to arrive.

> **VAREK:** It has stopped. I have you.

## Page 7 — Open air

**1.** Kaelen's chisel touches a rivet beside Ansi's cheek. Ansi flinches.

> **KAELEN:** Only the fastening, lad.

**2.** The mask lifts. Its black edges withdraw from the picture. Kaelen supports Ansi's head as the feeding apparatus is freed.

**3.** Ansi's uncovered face. He is a young man now. The tube has worn a notch inside his lower lip. His mouth tries to close around its absence. A worker puts a folded coat beneath the broken arm.

**4.** Kaelen works under the old wrist restraint.

> **KAELEN:** How long?
>
> **ANSI:** Five years.

Kaelen looks at him, then returns to the fastening.

## Page 8 — Lower Four

**One page only.** Four wide panels allow time to pass without reproducing the nursery's logistical sequence.

**1.** Pails pass along the boards. Ansi sits beside the other freed miner, holding a folded blanket beneath his head. Nearby, workers gather around the opened rescue frame. His good hand is occupied with useful work.

**2.** Later. From beside Varek's cradle, Sanna answers the old man's question.

> **SANNA:** The two men are out of their frames. One has a broken wrist.

Ansi remains with the injured miner. This is the continuity checkpoint before his departure.

**3.** Another worker takes Ansi's place. A woman helps him into the coat that supported his arm, settling his bound wrist inside it. In the background, carrying bars are prepared for Varek.

**4.** Ansi moves with evacuees toward the wash passage. People make room for the cradle. He checks each side opening as he walks. His search follows his established later presence; its exact route remains a pilot addition.

## Page 9 — The fourth note

**1.** Ansi stops outside a service gallery at a valve's **CLACK-HISS**. A handler stands at a brass control box. A marked control line runs toward a gripping frame.

**2.** The handler moves a lever. The powered gauntlet closes around a timber brace. Its cuff pulls the occupant's elbow against a stop. This establishes imposed grip before it reaches a person.

**3.** The lever returns. The gauntlet opens. The handler goes farther along the gallery to inspect a coupling. The frame's number plate is visible: **9**.

**4.** The eye inside its mask finds Ansi. Three notes emerge through the feeding apparatus. The fourth breaks into a cough.

**5.** Ansi stands in the doorway. The people ahead continue without him.

> **ANSI:** Nasa?

## Page 10 — His face

**1.** Ansi touches the edge of her mask. Nasa looks over his uncovered face, the marks beneath his jaw, and the hair grown unevenly around the old helmet.

**2.** Her thumb moves inside the open iron finger. He brings his cheek to it. She rubs at a streak of dirt, the way she might before either frame existed. Allow this to be a large, quiet panel.

**3.** He opens his eyes.

> **ANSI:** I am out.

Her eye creases. For a moment they share a smile.

**4.** She sees the bound wrist. He turns it toward the light for her. Her eye moves past him to the doorway, checking for anyone else. He follows the look. Nobody has come with him.

## Page 11 — Together: restraint

**A four-panel grid. Preserve the layout, viewing angles, and hand positions for page 12.** Ansi's borrowed coat now lies folded beneath the frame; its preparation is withheld until page 16.

**1 — Upper left.** Ansi reaches toward a large fitting. Nasa's thumb stops him against his sleeve. Her eye and fingertip indicate a smaller fastening beneath her shoulder brace. He finds it with the spanner.

**2 — Upper right.** She presses his sleeve again. In the depth of the same panel, the handler has turned toward them. Ansi holds the tool still.

**3 — Lower left.** The handler looks away. Nasa releases her thumb. Ansi turns the tool; the fastening gives. She shifts carefully as the strap loosens.

**4 — Lower right.** Their faces. She tests the new movement, bringing her shoulder forward a finger's width. He feels it. They look at each other and smile.

## Page 12 — Together: load

**Repeat page 11's grid and compositions. This is the deliberate second attempt, not an additional escape sequence.**

**1 — Upper left.** Ansi sets the tool at the load-bearing coupling pin. Nasa shifts within the loosened restraint to ease the strain. They wait until they are ready together.

**2 — Upper right.** Her thumb presses his sleeve as he brings his broken wrist against the tool. He removes it. The handler remains in the background, occupied at the coupling.

**3 — Lower left.** Ansi resets his boots and pulls with his good arm. Her shoulder shifts. The assembly moves slightly; the pin does not come free. A small inset memory of Varek supporting his frame can sit within this panel, without interrupting the later killing.

**4 — Lower right.** Their faces, matching the preceding page. The smile has gone. She looks at his binding. He is trying to recover his breath.

## Page 13 — Leave

**1.** Nasa sees the handler straighten. Her thumb presses Ansi's sleeve. Her eye goes toward the passage.

**2.** Ansi tries the pin again. She presses harder. He stops.

**3.** He looks into her face. A tear has reached the raw edge of the mask. Her gaze returns to the doorway, then to him. He understands what she is asking.

**4.** Close on both faces. Ansi shakes his head. His good hand settles over hers.

> **ANSI:** Wait.

No threat insert ends this page. Let the page turn interrupt them.

## Page 14 — Seize

**Three panels. No flashback, explanatory caption, or sudden change in musical feeling.**

**1.** The handler sees the loosened restraint and works the seizure control. The valve clicks. Use the control and imposed movement established on page 9.

**2.** The arm jerks forward. The powered fingers close around Ansi's throat. He catches her wrist with his good hand. She is pulling against the cuff too; her thumb strains outward inside its iron loop. Their eyes meet. He sees her trying.

**3.** The piston completes its short travel. A crack. His knees give way. The broken hand cannot grip. His good arm stretches toward the low shutoff and stops short. The device carries his weight. Final blocking must establish the valve's location and reachable distance before this is drawn as mechanically final.

## Page 15 — No fault found

**1.** The handler returns the lever. The gauntlet opens. Ansi drops beneath it, his good hand remaining short of the shutoff.

**2.** Nasa tries to bend toward him. The harness holds her upright. She can no longer see all of his face.

**3.** Her boot moves toward his hand until the frame's stop prevents it. Outside, people hurry past with pails. Her mouth works around the tube.

> **NASA:** An—

**4.** The handler checks the control response and the loose fitting. The service record is visible beside the control box. Ansi remains in the edge of the composition. The handler's inspection concerns whether the mechanism obeyed its controls.

**5.** His stylus completes the equipment entry. The wording and lettering match page 1 exactly:

**FRAME 9. NO FAULT FOUND.**

## Page 16 — Earlier

**1.** Small caption: **EARLIER.** Nasa has just recognized Ansi. Both pins remain seated; the spanner is still on the shelf. He takes off the borrowed coat. Its sleeve catches on the wrist binding. He works it loose carefully.

**2.** He folds the coat, kneels, and places it where he intends to lower her head. He presses his palm into it to test the thickness, then makes another fold. She watches him.

**3.** A large final panel: he looks up at her from beside the place he has prepared. Her gauntlet is open. No closing explanation or return to the death. If animated, cut to black after holding this image; the siblings' remembered work-song can finish its phrase there.
