# The Thulans
*Feature Screenplay Treatment | 90 Minutes*

---

### Overview

*The Thulans* is a contained industrial science fiction drama set inside a closed, subterranean mountain civilization that has survived three centuries on rationed air, coal, and scrap metal. 

The story follows Varek, an elderly pilot sealed inside a walking, three-tonne industrial caisson. After wrecking his rig to rescue forty miners from a planned cave-in, he discovers that the ruling bureaucracy knew the collapse was coming—and allowed it to meet a copper quota for an evacuation fleet that can only carry a tiny fraction of the population. Realizing that violence against the clerks cannot change the math, Varek descends to the flooded lower drifts and allows the miners to dismantle his life-support suit with shop tools so its parts can save their children.

---

### Characters

* **Varek Fairgunjis (300):** A veteran pilot bolted inside a walking steam-and-hydraulic caisson. His left arm carries a heavy demolition maul; his right carries an experimental magnetic load-coupler. He speaks through a bronze resonator plate set into his trachea. Under the iron, he is seventy pounds of withered skin, bedsore gristle, and cataract blindness.
* **Sanna (18):** Caretaker in the Lower Four communal nursery. Practical, blunt, and exhausted. Her older brother Wulfila volunteered for the Life Tithe forty days ago to earn the family a ration exemption; now she is fighting to keep six infants and her younger brother Bram alive in a flooded drift.
* **Bram (14):** Sanna and Wulfila’s younger brother. Drafted into the deep copper drifts despite Wulfila's sacrifice to meet an emergency quota; his leg is crushed during the collapse of Shaft 44.
* **Haila (19):** Wulfila's betrothed, who braided the cord and gave him the iron hammer-ring on the tithe platform. She now works with Sanna in the Lower Four nursery, refusing to let the family starve.
* **Kaelen (45):** A master smith in Lower Hearth Four. Pragmatic, soot-stained, armed with shop spanners and pipe tongs. His grandfather forged the original hip-pins in Varek's chassis.
* **Archon Theodemir (70):** Head of the Ministry of Continuity. He works out of an unheated stone room on Tier Nine, dressed in threadbare wool, managing casualty manifests and scrap allocations with an acoustic stylus and slate tablets.
* **Junior Surveyor Malakor (22):** A low-ranking ministry clerk stationed at the safety bulkheads. He knows the regulations by heart, but has never seen an actual mine collapse until Shaft 44 shears.

### Treatment

#### Act One: Shaft 44 (00:00 – 45:00)

**1. Drift Nine (00:00 – 12:00)**  
The film opens in near-darkness. Acoustic telegraph tickers punch paper tape in an iron relay box. Junior Surveyor Malakor stands watch at Bulkhead 44 on Tier Seven. Through the stone floor comes a high, dry whine: acoustic shear in the deep copper drifts three miles below. 

The speaking tube rattles. Trapped timbermen in Shaft 44 report that the roof-beams are bowing and the main ore shaker has pinned fourteen-year-old Bram by the leg. 

The wall telegraph clatters with instructions from Tier One: `BYPASS VENTILATION DOGS // PRESERVE FURNACE DRAFT`. If Malakor obeys, the smelters on Tier Four keep their blast air, but forty men in Shaft 44 suffocate in black-damp within forty minutes. Malakor hesitates at the heavy brass dogs of the bulkhead, terrified of the penalty for disobeying a sealed manifest.

**2. The Approach (12:00 – 22:00)**  
The passage vibrates with heavy, uneven footfalls: *thump, drag, hiss*. 

Varek emerges through the sulfur haze. His three-tonne iron rig is scarred by decades of rockfalls, streaked with lime-scale and dark hydraulic oil. In his left fist he drags the Fault Maul, its counter-pick furrowing the ballast rock. His right arm is an open mechanical sleeve housing the *Faírg-Tygil*—three hydraulic talons surrounding a copper induction coil.

Malakor begins reciting the emergency bypass protocol. Varek doesn't answer. He grips the bulkhead’s manual dog with his left gauntlet and twists. The two-inch steel spindle shears with a loud crack. Varek ducks his helmet under the lintel and enters the unventilated drift.

**3. The Shear (22:00 – 34:00)**  
Inside Shaft 44, the air is thick with slate dust. Main timbers are splintering along the grain. Bram is trapped beneath an eight-tonne slab of fallen basalt; Sanna is kneeling in the mud beside him, trying to stop the bleeding from his crushed shin with dirty rags. Kaelen and three timbermen are levering six-foot pinch bars under the slab, but the iron bars are bending into arcs.

Varek reaches the face. Every step shakes the brittle shale underfoot. As the central roof beam snaps, the ceiling begins a progressive collapse.

**4. The Load (34:00 – 45:00)**  
Varek steps over the injured boy. He drives the pick of his maul into the footwall to anchor his left side, plants his boots into the floor, and levels his right arm at the falling slab. 

His auxiliary diesel engine kicks in, coughing black exhaust into the ceiling pocket. The talons bite into the rock face and the copper coils spin up, whining into a high acoustic pitch. 

The *Faírg-Tygil* does not eliminate weight; it couples the kinetic load directly into Varek’s own chassis. The strain is immediate and mechanical: his right elbow casing twists three degrees out of alignment; a high-pressure line bursts, spraying hot amber oil across his visor; his right boot sinks several inches into the fractured floor. Inside the suit, Varek’s collarbone cracks under the compressive force.

"Clear the drift," Varek rasps through his bronze neck-plate.

Sanna, Kaelen, and the timbermen haul Bram and the other injured men through the safety dog into the bypass flume. As the last man clears the frame, the copper coils overheat and fuse into a lump of black slag. Varek heaves the rock aside with his failing hydraulics and drops to one knee as the drift caves in behind them.

Sitting in the dust, Varek finds Malakor’s discarded dispatch ribbon in the mud. He deciphers the punch marks: the Ministry knew the fault line would shear nineteen days ago, but kept the shift underground to hit the month's copper target.

Varek gets to his feet, leaves the miners behind, and begins the long climb toward the Spire.

#### Act Two: The Restitution (45:00 – 90:00)

**5. The Ledgers on Tier Nine (45:00 – 56:00)**  
Varek forces open the pine door of Archon Theodemir’s office on Tier Nine. The room is bare stone, furnished with wooden benches and rows of slate tablets. 

Theodemir does not call for guards. Without looking up, he places two tablets on the bench:
1. The Shaft 44 acoustic trace, dated nineteen days prior, noting an expected casualty budget of 38 to 45 hewers to meet the copper quota for Ship Four.
2. An older slate from Azg-Haíms: the hydrologic sounding confirming the North Well had eleven million gallons of water forty days before nineteen-year-old Wulfila volunteered for the Life Tithe.

Varek's throat plate hums: "Wulfila volunteered for nothing."

Varek remembers the platform at Azg-Haíms: the Ministry of Faith had ordered Varek to stand behind the boy, using three tonnes of ancestral iron to sanctify the death. Varek had stood silent while Haila tied the hammer-ring around Wulfila's neck, believing the boy was saving his people. Now he realizes the state murdered Wulfila to prevent a timber strike—and drafted his younger brother Bram into the deep shafts anyway.

Theodemir explains the arithmetic without raising his voice. If the district had known there was water, they would have demanded shorter shifts. The timber flumes would have dried up. The smelters would have chilled, and the plates for the evacuation ships would never be rolled. 

Theodemir draws a simple diagram on the wall slate: 4.2 million surviving Thulans living inside infrastructure built for forty-two million; the mantle rising forty yards a year; six ships under construction; six hundred thousand berths in total. Three point six million people cannot leave. Theodemir asks Varek who possesses the authority to choose which fourteen percent constitute the future of humanity.

"Kill me if you want," Theodemir says. "A deputy takes this bench at four o'clock. The numbers don't change because you have a hammer."

Varek looks at the slate, then down at the tool in his hand. For three hundred years, every problem had been something he could hit. This isn't.

He opens his fingers. The thirty-kilo Fault Maul drops to the floorboards, biting into the dry pine. He turns toward the stairs.

"Where are you going?" Theodemir asks.

"Down."

**6. Lower Four (56:00 – 66:00)**  
Varek descends to Lower Hearth Four—the tenement drift where Wulfila’s surviving family lives. In an unheated sub-drift, the communal nursery is flooding. The four-inch bilge pump has seized, its cast-iron casing split and its bronze impeller ground smooth by silt. Six infants lie in split dynamite crates as ditch water rises toward the straw. Bram lies beside them, feverish, his broken leg splinted with rough laths; Sanna and Haila are wringing out vinegar cloths in the dark.

Varek enters the drift, his right arm dead, his left knee dragging. He steps up to the ditch, grips the secondary hydraulic return line on his hip, and wrenches it free. Brass shears; hot mineral oil spills into the muck. He drops the auxiliary rotary pump at Kaelen’s boots.

"Four-inch line," Varek says. "It fits."

Kaelen warns him that bleeding the stabilizer will lock his knee. Varek sits down heavily on a pile of rotting larch ties in the dry corner. His leg stiffens as the remaining oil drains out.

"Bring the wrenches," he says.

**7. The Stripping (66:00 – 78:00)**  
Thirty miners crowd into the drift with cold chisels, hacksaws, and wooden grease tubs. They dismantle the caisson like a broken locomotive:
* Mineral oil is caught in wooden firkins; women soak rags in it to keep the nursery lamps burning.
* Heavy rolled-steel shin plates are unbolted and dragged off to reinforce sagging timbers at Station Six.
* An air scrubber is removed, but its round bayonet collar won't mate with the square flues on Tier Six; during an argument it drops on the stones, cracks, and vents three centuries of compressed mountain air into the ditch in seconds.
* In the passage, two women brawl over a copper elbow to trade for dried ling until Kaelen breaks them up.

Kaelen pulls the suit’s lead-lined wool liner from the chest—warm with residual engine heat—and drops it into Sanna’s lap for Bram. Sanna looks at her brother, whose teeth are clicking with cold, then walks past him and lays the heavy wool across the three dynamite crates to keep the infants from dying of croup. Bram watches her, pulls his thin gray blanket to his chin, and shuts his eyes.

Kaelen climbs behind Varek’s neck to unbolt the primary life-support collar. He warns Varek that once the seal breaks, he will be breathing raw mine air.

"Who made the hip pins?" Varek asks.

"My grandfather," Kaelen says.

"The iron was never mine. Take them off."

**8. The Hand (78:00 – 84:00)**  
By dawn, the suit is an empty heap of scrap on the mud floor. 

Sitting on the ties is a seventy-pound old man in shredded linen rags. His legs are white, withered, and scarred with deep purple indentations from centuries of clamp pressure. The eighty-pound helmet rests upside down in the mud like an old washbasin.

Down in the ditch, the auxiliary pump hums smoothly. The water level is dropping.

Sanna brings a tin mug of warm water from the kettle, flavored with boiled wild leeks and pine needles. She holds it to his lips. He swallows twice, most of it spilling into his beard.

Varek looks at his bare, liver-spotted right hand. Free of the steel gauntlet, his fingers shake with cold and palsy. 

Sanna sits on a nail keg beside him, reaches out with her scarred, carbolic-burned fingers, and takes his hand.

His fingers close over hers. They are brittle and light. Through her palm, he feels the warmth of living skin.

"You're cold," she says.

Varek looks at the water receding in the ditch, then leans his head back against the granite.

"No," he says. "It's all right."

He stops breathing.

**9. The Aftermath (84:00 – 90:00)**  
The miners bury Varek’s body three hundred feet below in an abandoned tool pocket, covering it with loose shale and leaving no marker.

Three days later, Ministry augment-enforcers sweep Lower Four looking for stolen components. Junior Surveyor Malakor heads the inspection. He kneels by the dry trench with a brass listening horn and clearly hears the smooth hum of Varek’s pump running under three feet of black mud.

He looks up and meets Sanna’s gaze. He sees the children breathing, Bram resting in clean straw. 

The sergeant asks if he hears machinery. Malakor takes his stylus and writes on the wax slate: `SWEEP COMPLETE // RECOVERABLE ASSETS: NONE`. He tells the squad the drift is dead shale and leads them out.

Six years later. 

On Tier One, the Council unveils a twenty-four-foot bronze statue of Varek in the Great Concourse before the embarkation gates of *The Ark of Continuity*. He is depicted as a muscular titan holding the Fault Maul overhead, with an inscription praising his selfless sacrifice for the state. Schoolchildren in gray tunics recite pledges beneath his bronze boots.

Three miles below, in the dark of Lower Four, a nine-year-old boy sits by the nursery wash-tank, looking at a scarred iron pipe-elbow holding the copper line together.

"Mother," the boy asks, "did that man die for the Spire?"

Sanna wipes the rim of her soup pot clean with a rag, sets it down, and looks at the iron fitting.

"No," she says. "It was our iron anyway."

### Production Notes

* **Tone & Realism:** No clean sci-fi surfaces or magic energy shields. Everything is cast iron, red rust, mineral oil, wet wool, and coal smoke. The mechanical components must look heavy, awkward, and prone to seizing.
* **Audio Strategy:** The soundscape transitions from loud mechanical violence in Act One (the metallic shriek of shearing rock, high-pressure oil leaks, diesel engines) to quiet, granular foley in Act Two (wrenches slipping on rusted bolts, the bubbling cough of an old man's chest, the low hum of a pump buried in mud).
* **Sets:** Over 80% of the film takes place in three functional, claustrophobic environments: Bulkhead 44, the Archon’s stone office, and the Lower Four communal nursery.
