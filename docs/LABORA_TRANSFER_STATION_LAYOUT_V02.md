When released/open, the **drop shutter is raised overhead**, leaving an actual clear floor passage. The view is a *location marker*, not a falsely closed wall. Edda and Pell cross to an exit on the operating-gallery side, away from the active arm.

## Load path

**Gravity shutter → chain → drum → manual brake → fixed station frame.** The manual brake must support the shutter *independently of Nessa*. Ansen holds it while Nessa uses only a few millimetres of permitted shoulder/torso movement to shift a **catch alignment link** (the previous **20 mm** allowance is an unverified hypothesis). Catch seats in the raised shutter. The workers cross. No magical strength or extra control over the powered arm is granted to Nessa.

## Three states

**A — Work / closure.** Edda is injured and Pell remains beyond the shutter. Steam is being isolated and shutter drops toward closure. Nessa sees the catch is misaligned; Hesk is occupied at the adjacent drive.

**B — Joint rescue.** Ansen arrests shutter descent with brake; Nessa trims link alignment; catch engages; Pell and Edda pass. Three visual actions, not a long heist.

**C — Seizure.** Hesk returns, detects interference, orders Ansen back. Ansen steps beyond the **normal** working envelope. Hesk deliberately switches to **wide direct-manual** sweep (foreshadow in an earlier work beat), commands the gripper against Ansen, and Nessa's resistance cannot stop it. Final image belongs only to her and his body.

## Proof gates — do not script as verified physics

1. Demonstrate brake/load capacity and independent retention of falling shutter.
2. Trace Nessa's limited voluntary motion to the catch; specify actual trim travel and joint constraints.
3. Establish both ordinary and wide sweep with believable arms, valve/cam/clutch travel and reach.
4. Mark Hesk's sightline, Ansen's start/retreat/exit, gripping finger span and fatal contact point.
5. Resolve timing of Hesk's mode change, Nessa's warning and the commanded stroke.
6. Show uninterrupted worker escape path and station access in the novella's short clock.
7. Show resistance and shoulder injury without turning the impact into extended spectacle.

**Script-lock acceptance:** The rescue and death are legible with no balloons, and a reviewer can trace physical load and control. Until then, this is **blocking V0.2 — not tested engineering**.
