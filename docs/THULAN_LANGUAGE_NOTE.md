# THULAN LANGUAGE NOTE
### *Names, Roots, and Address Grammar*

*Companion to [`THE_THULANS_LORE_BIBLE_v3.md`](THE_THULANS_LORE_BIBLE_v3.md) §0.
Governed by RULING 014 (Naming & Linguistic Standard) and RULING 015 (Archaic Latin
Mottos).*

---

## 1. The Keystone Terms

- ***Thulan*** — the people, and the honored title ("Thulan of the Old Hearths"). Root:
  attested Gothic verb ***þulan*** "to endure, bear, suffer" (cf. Old English *þolian*,
  German *dulden*). The nominalized agent form used in-world is a labeled Thulan
  descendant (T3), **not** an attested Gothic noun. The whole thematic axis — *"ENDURE
  FOR"* vs *"ENDURE THE"* — rests on this root.
- ***Fairguni*** — the Mountain (the setting itself). Attested Gothic ***fairguni***
  "mountain" (T1). The genitive ***Fairgunjis*** ("of the Mountain") is the ceremonial
  dynastic form, e.g. *Varek Fairgunjis*.

## 2. The Tier Ladder

| Tier | Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1 — Attested** | A real, attested Gothic name or word used as-is. | *Wulfila*, *Fritigern*, *Fairguni* |
| **T2 — Adapted** | An attested Gothic root or name lightly smoothed for modern eyes. | *Haila* ← *hails*; *Watona* ← *wato* |
| **T3 — Invented** | A new descendant form built from real Gothic roots, explicitly labeled as a fictional evolution. | *Garuni* ← *garuni* "counsel / plan"; *Thulan* ← *þulan* |
| **T4 — Modern Drift** | A modern lower-drift vernacular name, drifted from attested Gothic or absorbed from contact dialects over 3,000 years. Permitted for modern characters only; not presented as attested Gothic and carrying no classical root claim. | *Sanna*, *Bram*, *Kaelen*, *Malakor*, *Jarek*, *Tomas*, *Halza* |

**Provenance rule:** every T2/T3 form carries a one-line root note. T4 forms carry no
root claim and must not be presented as historically attested Gothic.

### Salvation names — approved 27 October 2026

- **Ansi** (AHN-see) — **T2 adapted name**, from Gothic *ans*, "beam / rafter," the author's mine-timber association. The adapted personal name is not claimed as an attested Gothic name.
- **Nasa** (NAH-sah) — **T3 fictional Thulan name**, derived from Gothic *nasjan*, "to save." This is an invented descendant name, not an attested Gothic personal name.
- **The Thulans: Salvation** is the English working title. *Ganis* was considered and rejected; it is not her name or an established in-world word.

See [Salvation story direction](SALVATION_STORY_DIRECTION.md). These siblings are a separate household, not additions to Wulfila's family core.

## 3. Address Grammar

- **Ordinary address:** `[Name] of [Hearth/place]` — e.g. *Wulfila of Azg-Haíms*.
- **Formal / ceremonial register:** the genitive **-jis** pattern extends to dynastic
  titles — e.g. *Wulfila Azghaimis*, *Varek Fairgunjis*.
- **No generic fantasy surnames.** *Ashwake, Blackstone, Ironborn, Emberfall* are
  prohibited without a specific cultural reason.

## 4. Kinship Frame (Locked)

- ***Giba-Broþrans*** — the oath-and-gift brotherhood; ***giba*** "gift" (attested) +
  ***broþar*** "brother" (attested).
- ***swistar*** — "sister" (attested); used in the kinship frame.
- **The family core (RULING 029):** Wulfila (19); his siblings **Sanna (18)** and
  **Bram (13 during the main action; turns 14 on 30 October)**; **Haila**, Wulfila's betrothed. Sanna — not Haila — is Wulfila's sister.

## 5. Archaic Latin Layer (RULING 015)

- ***FORTIS ET LIBER*** ("Strong and Free") — stamped into the collar of the Fault Maul
  (*Faírguni-Hamars*).
- ***ORA ET LABORA*** ("Pray and Work") — stamped into the inner plate behind the
  Gren-Skildus.
- Archaic Latin is an older, pre-cataclysm disaster-engineering inheritance from Varek's
  own era. Modern miners and Council members no longer speak or understand it; it survives
  only as battered, stamped fossil inscriptions on his tools and inner chassis. This
  layering (Gothic substrate + older Latin fossils) is intentional, not an inconsistency.
