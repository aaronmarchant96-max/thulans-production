## Visual lock — Bram, six years later

- **Age in scene:** approximately 19, following the six-year epilogue after the main-story period when he is 13 (turning 14).
- **Face:** lean, angular youthful face; clean-shaven, strong dark eyebrows, guarded serious expression. Keep the approved face consistent through front, three-quarter, profile and rear views.
- **Hair:** short, dark, uneven rough-cropped top with closely cut back and sides, as shown in the approved multi-view sheet. This is Bram's approved cut, **not** a new universal rule for Thulan culture.
- **Clothes:** plain, repeatedly worn/repaired lower-workings work clothes, dark collar/scarf and work straps; no ornamental armour, aristocratic styling or fantasy-costume flourishes.
- **Visual treatment:** gritty near-black/charcoal ink-grain graphic-novel finish, practical industrial environment, limited motivated warm amber.
- **Avoid:** fashionable swept hero hair, generic fantasy protagonist styling, aging him into a bearded middle-aged man, silently changing identity across panels, or transplanting a separate actor's face.

## Narrative boundaries / handoff

- **Source hierarchy:** The authoritative illustrated Google Doc and current `RULINGS.md` govern events and ages. This is an **art reference approval**, not a prose rewrite or new story event.
- Bram kept and safeguarded the signed order; six years later the unnamed foundry worker (provisionally called Trigg in development) asks for a copy and Bram says, “We'll read it together.” That extension remains separate from any unapproved subsequent scene.
- The original broken grey roofing slate with hardened red crossed-calipers tally-wax, the signed order, and later copies/repair slips are **distinct physical objects**. Never render an invented sealed envelope as a substitute.
- No approval here for the other character's facial design, new script pages, a universal Suebian/Norman hairstyle convention, or title/faction changes.
- One artwork panel at a time: supply canon packet and approved reference; check ages, props, continuity, and legibility; assemble in Canva only after panel approval.
