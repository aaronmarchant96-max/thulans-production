## C. Script/blocking proof tests

1. **Clock test (PASS only with map):** Nessa's station lies on a *usable, short spur* from a wash passage active in Chapter Ten. No crossing the already closed Tier Five route. On-panel elapsed time stays bounded by the approaching closure.
2. **Power test (PASS only with circuit diagram):** Isolating the drop-shutter hoist does not simultaneously isolate Hesk's powered grip. Draw separate labeled branches or a realistic retained-pressure system. Do not assert an unspecified accumulator exists.
3. **Load test (PASS only with sections):** Gravity shutter → chain/drum → load-holding brake/retaining pawl → station frame. Nessa never carries shutter weight.
4. **Human control test (preferred alternate):** Nessa's signal is a previously established working habit; Ansen can interpret it by observing or through a one-line practical instruction, without perfect speech through the mask.
5. **Visibility test:** Nessa can observe the catch engagement; Ansen at the brake cannot. Pell and Edda can pass clear of the arm, and no phantom barrier remains in the raised door opening.
6. **Wide sweep test:** On an early work panel show Hesk selecting the wide manual envelope for a mundane heavy lift. At the death he selects the *same* mode; Ansen has not seen it. Plan and elevation establish reach and a constrained escape corridor.
7. **Resistance test:** Show frame arm motion forcibly transmitted into Nessa's shoulder; her opposition injures her but cannot mechanically interrupt the commanded stroke. If these forces do not align in rig, change injury staging rather than invent dynamics.
