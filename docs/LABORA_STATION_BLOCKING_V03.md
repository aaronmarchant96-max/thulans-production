# LABORA — transfer-gallery blocking V0.3

**29 October 2026 · AUTHOR-ACCEPTED STORY BLOCKING / NOT ENGINEERING CERTIFICATION**

**Scope:** A **simple reader-believability sketch**, not a machine-design project. This is the preferred working alternative to [V0.2](LABORA_TRANSFER_STATION_LAYOUT_V02.md) and follows the [live-manuscript continuity review](LABORA_TIMELINE_AND_MECHANICS_REVIEW_V01.md). No final title, page count, illustration, mechanical dimensions or novella changes.

## Plan — where people stand

```text
FAR WORK GALLERY              SHUTTER                OPERATING GALLERY → EXIT

Edda (hurt) + Pell  → →       | raised |        BRAKE [Ansen works this]
                             |        |         Nessa/Frame 9
                             |        |            ↘  normal working arc
                             |        |                ↘ WIDE commanded reach
                             |        |                       Ansen after retreat
                             |        |                   Hesk at manual controls
```

- With the shutter **raised**, the floor passage is clear. Edda and Pell cross to safety before the seizure.
- Ansen is close enough to operate the manual **load-holding** brake using his **good** hand. The station structure, not his body, holds shutter weight.
- Nessa can **see or hear** when the retaining catch is ready; she signals Ansen. He eases the shutter against the catch until it seats. This replaces V0.2's unsupported shoulder-to-catch mechanism.
- Ansen then returns to Nessa. Hesk discovers the interference. Ansen backs beyond the **ordinary** work arc, but remains within the arm's **wider manual-command** reach.

## Elevation — what one drawing must show

```text
     FIXED SUPPORT / HOIST
         drum
          |             Nessa upright in frame
     [raised shutter]       [shoulder] ──── gripper ── Ansen
          |                  (arm reaches throat height)
   FREE FLOOR PASSAGE          frame feet             Ansen feet
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

**Two important conditions**, and no additional calculations needed for story draft:

1. The shutter's hoist can be isolated while Nessa's powered frame **still has motive pressure**; otherwise Hesk could not command its final sweep.
2. The wider manual reach is established **once in an ordinary work scene** before Hesk uses it against Ansen. Ansen has not seen that mode; Nessa recognizes it.

## Page-readable sequence

**Rescue:** Ansen holds brake → Nessa gives two short work hums at the right moment → Ansen eases brake, catch seats → Edda and Pell cross.

**Tragedy:** Hesk returns → orders Ansen back → Ansen steps out of the ordinary work arc → Hesk selects the already-established wider manual mode and commands the grip → Nessa resists, but the machine kills Ansen.

**Ending:** Hesk leaves. Nessa remains fixed upright over Ansen's body. **Nothing is said.**

## Stop rule

The following are **deliberately not specified**: machine mass, exact lever ratios, hydraulic or steam pressure, pawl tooth profile, shoulder dislocation force, fixed coordinates, precise actuator timing, and frame model.

This scene passes the **story-level spatial test** if a storyboard can make the above actions unambiguous. If an artist discovers that an action cannot be drawn without a contradiction, fix that **single** action. **Do not launch a separate engineering project** to validate a fictional shutter.

**Continuity:** Place the short spoken lead and transfer-station detour **after Ansen's onstage Chapter Ten assistance**. His Chapter Eleven mention is reported information, not an additional physical appearance; the transfer gallery must be reachable from a still-open wash-passage spur inside the closing evacuation clock.
