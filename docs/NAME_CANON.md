# THULANS NAME CANON & REGISTERS (DRAFT)

Two registers govern player-facing text and in-world texture:
1. **Hearth Tongue (Vernacular)**: Player-facing titles, roster, UI, and dialogue. Smoothed T2 and T4 modern drift. Pronounceable on sight by English speakers.
2. **Record-Speech (Gothic)**: Inscriptions, ritual, and Council bureaucracy. T1–T3 roots. Never explained on first contact.
