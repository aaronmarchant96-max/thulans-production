# THULAN NOVELLA VOICE CONTRACT & PROSE AUDIT SPEC
*Authority: Aaron Marchant | Canon Boundary: RULINGS.md, GDR_SLOP.md*

---

## 1. The Core Law: "Deep Line-Editing" Over Prompt Generation

No chapter or scene is final when an LLM spits it out. Raw generation is only raw gravel; the story is cut with cold chisels. 

Every manuscript pass must shift from "generating and tweaking" to **forensic line-editing**. If a sentence sounds like it was synthesized in Silicon Valley, it gets cut. If a paragraph doesn't have dirt, oil, or blood under its fingernails, it gets rewritten from the boots up.

---

## 2. The Blacklist: Banned "AI Slop" Vocabulary

The following words, tropes, and rhythmic tics are **strictly prohibited** in all Thulan prose, dialogue, and descriptive text. If an audit detects any of these, the passage fails inspection:

| Category | Banned Terms & Clichés | Why It's Banned | Mandatory Replacement Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Puffed-Up Metaphors** | *tapestry, symphony, testament, beacon, dance, labyrinth, crescendo, crucible* | Generic LLM filler that sounds like a college essay. | Concrete physical objects: *iron rail, slag heap, grease firkin, hemp braid.* |
| **Fake Depth Words** | *delve, palpable, visceral, ethereal, eerie, profound, unspoken, unfathomable* | Lazy writer's shorthand trying to force emotion. | Describe the physical symptom: *cold sweat in eyebrows, teeth chattering on cold tallow, a cracked shin.* |
| **Sound & Motion Tics** | *hummed (used loosely), shuddered, danced, flickered with life, hung heavy* | AI's default sensory crutches. | Mechanical and physical verbs: *rattled, sheared, clanked, thumped, hissed, bit into.* |
| **Philosophical Inflation** | *"A testament to human resilience..." / "In this unforgiving world..." / "A stark reminder that..."* | Moralizing editorial narrator. | Let the action tell it. *Show the boy saving a drop of grease on his nail.* |

## 3. The Voice Contract: Style & Cadence

1. **Brutal, Short, Sensory (The McCarthy/Abercrombie Line):**
   - Sentences should land like an eight-pound sledge on granite.
   - Favor monosyllabic Anglo-Saxon root words and heavy physical verbs (*crack, shear, slam, drag, bite, thump*).
   - Avoid comma splices and wandering dependent clauses.
2. **The "Under-the-Fingernails" Rule:**
   - Every paragraph must be anchored in physical labor, material physics, or physiological reality.
   - Tools have names, sizes, and wear patterns (e.g., *thirty-millimeter hex nut, four-pound cross-peen hammer, eighteen-inch monkey wrench*).
   - Scarcity is tactile: lard is counted in drams; timber is measured in notched cap-pieces; warmth is three layers of lead-lined felt.
3. **Albertan Frontier Resentment:**
   - The prose reflects the honest, bitter reality of people who pull resources out of the frozen ground while distant bureaucrats in high towers treat them like line items on a spreadsheet.

## 4. Character Speech Registers & Vocabulary

Characters must never share the same vocabulary pool. Their diction is dictated by their trade, age, and trauma.

### A. Bram (Age 14 | Apprentice / Sump-Boy)
* **Register:** Hesitant, innocent, literal, observant.
* **Vocabulary:** Senses, immediate tasks, things his brother told him, childish misunderstandings of a surface he has never seen (*"beasts as big as tubs covered in hair"*).
* **Cadence:** Short sentences, questions that ask about reality rather than philosophy. He doesn't complain about pain; he just shivers and holds the tub.
* **Dialogue Rule:** Never let Bram speak with adult political cynicism. He worries about dropped bolts, cold toes, and whether his sister is coughing.

### B. Kaelen (Age 58 | Drift Smith / Veteran Hewer)
* **Register:** Cynical, exhausted, pragmatic, shop-floor curt.
* **Vocabulary:** Metal gauges, timber species (*larch, spruce*), tool weights, joint fatigue, trade shorthand, caustic frontier gallows humor.
* **Cadence:** Clipped imperatives, muttered curses around a tallow candle, zero patience for ideological nonsense or romantic nostalgia.
* **Dialogue Rule:** He hides deep communal protectiveness under rough insults (*"I'm not shorting the tally six ounces of scrap for your blue fingers"*). He never gives a speech; he gives instructions.

### C. Varek (Age 300+ | Ancestral Caisson / The Dismantled)
* **Register:** Heavy, ancient, monosyllabic, acoustic, dying engine.
* **Vocabulary:** Structural loads, acoustic frequencies, old measurements (*fathoms, leagues, hundredweight*), names of ancestral kin.
* **Cadence:** Deep vibration through a bronze throat-plate. Words are expensive because every syllable burns auxiliary air. When the suit is stripped, his voice is a seventy-pound old man's wheeze.
* **Dialogue Rule:** Maximum economy. Never argues. Drops the hammer instead of debating. Speaks the truth once, flat as a surveyor's slate: *"Bring the wrenches."*

### D. Sanna (Age 20 | Nursery Caretaker / Lower Four Hearth)
* **Register:** Clinical domestic triage, fierce maternal protectiveness, calm defiance.
* **Vocabulary:** Rations, flannel, carbolic wash, child fever, tea stems, iron scrap.
* **Cadence:** Unflinching, practical, quiet. No hysterics.
* **Dialogue Rule:** The moral anchor. When confronting the state or caring for the dying, she speaks with absolute finality: *"No. It was our iron anyway."*

## 5. Pacing & Dynamic Range (Avoiding One-Note Misery)

Grimdark fails when it is pure, unrelenting depression. The story must breathe and have dynamic range:

1. **Moments of Dark Gallows Humor:**
   - Miners mocking Council pronouncements.
   - Smiths making morbid bets on which timber prop will blow first.
   - The practical comedy of people making impossible machinery work with baling wire and spit.
2. **Warmth & Human Light ("Hope + Cost = Meaning"):**
   - Small domestic triumphs: a tin cup of hot leek water shared with a child; wiping grease off a cheek; a dried sprig of sun-pine pinned to an apron.
   - The reason men endure the drift is the person waiting by the coal stove in the lower tier.
3. **Sudden Kinetic Violence & Mechanical Catastrophe:**
   - Don't just talk about danger—detonate it.
   - A high-pressure steam union fractures; a haul cable snaps with the force of an artillery shell; an iron tooth binds in a winch gear; a bulkhead shears.
   - Action must be sharp, chaotic, and governed by industrial physics.

---

## 6. Pre-Flight Line-Editing Checklist

Before any chapter or scene draft is merged into canon:
- [ ] Has every instance of the Banned AI Vocabulary list been purged?
- [ ] Do Bram, Kaelen, Varek, and Sanna sound like four completely different people if you hide their character tags?
- [ ] Is there at least one concrete measurement, tool, or physical material in every paragraph?
- [ ] Does the scene contain a counterweight of human warmth or gallows humor to balance the grime?
- [ ] Is the mountain kept smaller than the human beings under it (Ruling 001)?
