# Salvation - episode specification

## Story and production scope

Salvation is canon. Its central events and penal-system premise are governed by [RULING 035](../../RULINGS.md#ruling-035-welded). The current production source is [pilot revision 3](../../docs/SALVATION_PILOT_16_PAGES.md), with 16 pages and 66 panels. This is a working presentation of the canonical story, not a finished production or a claim that the novella already contains these scenes.

Ansen and Nessa received three months for stealing bread from the upper Spire and remain confined five years later. Varek frees Ansen from a breaker rig. Ansen later tries to free his sister; the commanded gripping frame kills him while she resists. Nessa's eventual fate remains open.

## Feel and reader experience

- Let their sibling rivalry, humour, and working lives be enjoyable in their own right.
- Give their reunion genuine recognition and progress. Both have agency and try to protect the other.
- Under pressure, teasing gives way to private, direct attention. Their changed voices need no polished farewell.
- Preserve continuous pursuit and an inhabited place. Do not stop every action to explain its emotional meaning.
- Keep the fatal movement brief and mechanically legible. Let understanding and Nessa's attempts to respond take longer.
- The gentle song may continue through the killing. No automatic horror sting, tempo ramp, or cut to silence is required.

## Continuity

- Both men survive Varek's Chapter Six encounter. He kills the guard aiming at Bram, not Ansen.
- Ansen's Chapter Ten help and the Chapter Eleven confirmation remain intact. The search follows that presence; exact travel and elapsed time are unresolved.
- Nessa is a separate prisoner, not the second miner already freed.
- Varek's pilot line is the author-requested "It has stopped. I have you." The supplied manuscript's contraction remains unchanged in its source quotation.
- The handler, gallery, and Frame 9 events belong to Salvation canon. Final control geometry, line routing, and reachable distances remain to be blocked.
- The novella's six-year epilogue and Varek's death are unchanged. Eleven Bells does not override this story.

## Production prerequisites

The asset register requires 66 source-panel art assets plus dialogue, original song/score, and foley/atmosphere. Assets are currently missing. Layered artwork must distinguish Nessa's small voluntary gestures from imposed motion and support the restricted-view opening without an arbitrary crop.

No master runtime, individual shot duration, voice cast, final lyrics, pitch map, locked character design, or verified engineering dimensions are approved by this package. Proposed technical delivery is 24 fps and 1920x1080; do not import prototype playback rates or adjust voices to force an assumed runtime.
