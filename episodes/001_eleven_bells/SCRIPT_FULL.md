# Eleven Bells — Recording Script (Take 5)

**Format**: Narrated Motion Graphic Novel Teaser  
**Voice**: Aaron (Quiet gravitas, weary survivor recounting history)  
**Tone Reference**: *Gears of War* "Mad World" / *Dead Island* Reveal Trailer  
**Estimated Read Time**: 60 – 70 seconds  

---

## Performance Direction
* **Pacing**: Deliberate and unhurried. Do not perform or force an "announcer" voice. Speak it naturally, like you were there and you still smell the steam and grease.
* **The `[...]` Cues**: These are breath pauses. Let the silence hang for half a second so the violin and the iron bell can ring into the empty space.

---

## The Script

Twelve shield warriors held the throat of the lift,  
three hundred civilians jammed knee to knee in the cage, a dockhand with one crushed hand still wrapped around a dented enamel tin,  
backs pressed to the grating, boots in sump water, breath sour with coal dust and grease.

[Breath pause]

The men coming down the gallery weren't soldiers.  
They were the Welded—condemned miners bolted into hydraulic frames,  
jawbones pinned with rivets, eyelids stitched open on iron wire,  
ribs capped in plate, arms driven by steam cams that never rested.

[Breath pause]

Every time the lift came back for more, an iron bell struck.  
Each bell meant another line had failed above.

[Breath pause]

At the third bell, a pressure main burst in the ceiling,  
spitting boiling mist through the beams, slicking the walls white.

At the fifth... the haul cable tore free.  
It lashed across the shaft throat, took six warriors at once,  
armor split, shields knocked loose, bodies folded into the winch frame.

[Breath pause]

By the seventh bell, dead men were still upright in their harnesses—  
industrial shield hooks locked under the bodies' weight.  
And the Welded kept hammering at the corpses,  
steel fists ringing off dented helms.

[Breath pause]

On the final run, the hoist jammed.  
The last warrior hauled up his pneumatic hammer... stepped out of the cage...  
drove the steel head into the drive teeth so the safety doors could seal shut.

[Breath pause]

The eleventh bell rang from the shaft below,  
a hollow thrum that echoed the last breath of those still trapped.

[Long pause]

The Council later put a bronze plaque over the rusted throat of the lift, called it a monument.

In truth... it was just a repair they never completed.
