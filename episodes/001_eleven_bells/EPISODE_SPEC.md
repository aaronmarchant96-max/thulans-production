## 1. Premise & Logline

Twelve shield warriors hold an elevator throat against a deployment of **The Welded** (*Gathaur-Bano*—condemned miners welded into unstoppable hydraulic breaker frames) while approximately three hundred civilians funnel into the descending cage. Every time the lift returns, it rings an iron bell. Each bell marks the defence worsening as hydraulic violence and machinery failures compound, until only one warrior remains to make an irreversible mechanical choice.

---

## 2. Core Narrative Beats

1. **The Throat (0:00 – 0:08)**: Twelve shield warriors lock their plates at the elevator threshold. Three hundred civilians funnel into the descending cage as the heavy, wheezing clank of **The Welded** echoes from the dark gallery.
2. **The Cadence of Descent (0:08 – 0:12)**: The cage drops and returns. Each cycle rings the lift bell, marking time purchased against the relentless hydraulic breaker bars.
3. **The Third Bell (0:12 – 0:17)**: Pressure main ruptures under kinetic collision and acoustic stress. Steam and scalding mist choke the gallery, silhouetting the riveted spit-masks of the attackers.
4. **The Fifth Bell (0:17 – 0:28)**: Winding cable snaps under load, whips across the gallery, slicing cleanly through heavy aprons and fracturing hydraulic pistons. The machinery continues turning.
5. **The Seventh Bell (0:28 – 0:38)**: Dead shield warriors remain standing upright because their industrial shield hooks will not release under dead weight; the surviving Welded continue swinging mechanically on broken limbs.
6. **The Jammed Lift (0:38 – 0:46)**: On the final run, the cage jams in the throat. The last warrior pulls his piston hammer, steps out of the cage, and drives the hammer into the hoist gear so the doors can seal behind the civilians.
7. **The Eleventh Bell (0:46 – 0:50)**: The lift bell rings one final time from the shaft below.
8. **The Residue & Myth (0:50 – 0:55.83)**: Years later, the hammer remains embedded in the rusted hoist. The Council labels it a heroic monument; in truth, it is simply an unfinished repair over an industrial slaughter.

## 3. Physical Evidence Tokens

* **The Severed Aprons**: Heavy leather and boiled hides sliced clean by the whipping steel cable.
* **The Locked Shield Hooks**: Mechanical anchor pins jammed by lateral torque, keeping corpses upright.
* **The Piston Hammer**: Heavy pneumatic striking head wedged between the hoist spur gear and the collar teeth.
* **The Council Plaque**: Polished bronze plate mounted over rusted iron teeth that are still bent out of round.
