*Traditional Soot-Worker’s Chant of the Lower Hearths*

STATUS: CANONICAL LYRIC
RULING: 021
METER: 6/8 compound meter

## Canon Notes (RULING 021)
- **Perspective:** The Voice of the Lower Hearths & Soot-Pickers.
- **Authorship:** Collective oral folk memory; Haila is an associated performance voice.
- **Sonic Physics:** Diegetic labor progression: dry solo voice $\rightarrow$ pick on 1 & boot on 4 $\rightarrow$ shift harmony $\rightarrow$ mass shaft chorus and bedrock resonance.
- **Forensic Spine:** Varek's *"When was it surveyed?"* transformed into the public street-level accusation: *"Who knew? Who knew? / Say when you knew!"*
- **Final Whispered Line:** *"...they knew before he offered."*

## Canonical Master Lyrics (V2)

```text
[Stanza 1 — Solo Female Voice, Completely Dry in a Wet Drainage Shaft]
(No instruments. Only distant water drops on stone.)

Take the hammer to the seam,
strike the seam until it breaks.
Count the hours of the shift,
count the bread the council takes.
Cold well, cold well,
how long did they know?
We gave a brother to the dark
while water waited below.

[Stanza 2 — Single Worker Joins: Pick Strike on Beat 1, Boot Stomp on Beat 4]
(CLANG — — THUD — — | CLANG — — THUD — —)

Tie the cord around his neck,
send him walking to the stone.
Tell his mother that he died
just to bring the cistern home.
Cold well, cold well,
who signed away his name?
The valve was shut before he came;
they opened it the same.

[Stanza 3 — Shift Miners Join in Low Harmony; Chains in Triplets]
(Chains rattle: shhh-chink-chink | Heavy hammers on rail iron: CLANG! CLANG!)

Gold on every upper gate,
rust on every lower tier.
They were counting out the days,
they were trading on our fear.
Cold well, cold well,
how long was it known?
The water had a date and place
before he stood alone.

[Stanza 4 — Mass Shaft Chorus: Pounding Boots, Shouted Accusations, Bedrock Resonance]
(Thousands of voices shouting the counter-refrain; ventilation ducts resonating)

[VOICES IN ACCUSATION]
Who knew? Who knew?
Say when you knew!
Who knew? Who knew?
Say when you knew!

[MASS CHORUS — Full Resonant Power]
Take the mantle from the priest,
tear the ledger from the wall.
When the lie began to break,
not a stone began to fall.
Cold well, cold well,
now we know the cost:
The Mountain did not answer us—
we answered for the lost!

[Outro — Boots & Clangs Stop Abruptly. Only Echoing Room Tone. A Single Breath.]
(Whispered close to the microphone):
"...they knew before he offered."
```
