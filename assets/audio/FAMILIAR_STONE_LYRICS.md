# FAMILIAR STONE (THE WEIGHT OF A LIE)
*Official Teaser Trailer Theme & Ballad for The Thulans*

---

## Master Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
All around me are the soot-stained walls
Worn-out boots in the narrow halls
Early shift for the daily drill
Going nowhere... going downward still.
And their water cups are running dry
As the Council watches from on high
Children praying to an empty sky
Trading marrow for another lie.

[Chorus]
And I find it kind of heavy
I find it kind of cold
The greatest story ever written
Was the one our mothers told.
When the Mountain was a shelter
Not a butcher on the throne...
And a boy stepped in the dark
Just to get his sister home.

[Verse 2]
Iron valves in the golden tower
Count the blood to measure power
Hide the spring behind the heavy gate
Teach the poor that hunger is their fate.
They weld the plate around my chest
Tell the titan he will never rest
Three tons of iron... five leagues below...
With nowhere left for us to go.

[Chorus]
And I find it kind of heavy
I find it kind of cold
The greatest story ever written
Was the one our mothers told.
When the Mountain was a shelter
Not a butcher on the throne...
And a boy stepped in the dark
Just to get his sister home.

[Bridge - Spoken / Whispered]
I remember how her fingers felt.
Before the iron...
Before the stone.
No one told him that the well was full.
He went down there alone.

[Chorus - Emotional Peak]
And I find it kind of heavy
I find it kind of cold
The only god they ever gave us
Was a lie that they had sold.
Now the ceiling starts to fracture
Now the iron stands alone...
While a mother touches skin
That will never reach its home.

[Outro]
Never reach its home...
Just cold stone.
```
