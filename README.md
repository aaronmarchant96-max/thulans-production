### Narrative Synopsis
In the mountain city of Fairguni, water is rationed by blood. Forty days after nineteen-year-old Wulfila surrenders his life for his family's water allowance, his younger brother Bram finds Wulfila's brass chit hidden in a rail coupling. Guided by Wulfila's instructions, Bram and the drift smith Kaelen retrieve a survey slate from his locker: **the North Cistern was full two days before Wulfila died.**

Varek, an old man inside a three-tonne rescue machine, leaves his state post to protect the workers opening the water feeder. He goes to the official who can recall the approaching ram crews, expecting the evidence to compel a correction. The official already knows. Varek returns to Lower Four, where the workers remove his rescue pump for hand operation and care for the injured man inside the frame.

## LABORA — companion-story development (29 October 2026)

**Three months was the sentence. Five years later, they are still inside.**

The companion project was developed in this repository as *The Thulans: Salvation*. **LABORA** is now the favored **working title** and **FORTIS** remains an alternate; neither is a final published title or authorization to rename files globally.

**Ansen and Nessa are unrelated orphans who grew up together**, not siblings as in the older draft. They steal bread at roughly thirteen and receive three-month sentences inside separately assigned powered penal frames. Varek frees Ansen five years later. After his already-established Chapter Ten/Eleven participation, Ansen finds Nessa working a transfer station. Together they open a passage for trapped workers. When the handler **Hesk** commands her frame to seize Ansen, she cannot stop its powered grip from killing him. The closing image is silent: **Nessa trapped upright above Ansen's body**.

This is **approved creative direction, not a finished script or verified machine design**. **Hesk** (replacing Brun, too close to Bram), **Edda** and **Pell** are provisional companion-story names. Page count, full merged prose, chronology, blocking, artwork and cover remain open. The existing **16-page/66-panel Salvation revision 3** script is retained for comparison, not silently edited to match the new premise.

## The rebellion novellas

The planned continuation follows a rebellion rising through the Mountain toward the Spire. Each community faces its own problem: refugees needing shelter, loyalists protecting what they have, workers who cannot afford to miss a shift, and officials changing their public language as allegiances shift.

Reports of what happened below reach the next district before the rebellion does. The people receiving those reports already have households, obligations, and reasons to fear what comes next. The series explores how their choices change the rebellion and the lives that depend on the same water, air, food, and transport.

The state's monument appropriates Varek's defiance; Bram's invitation to read the preserved order together supplies a small organizational seed. Varek remains dead. Exact volume count, titles, floor numbering, sequel dates, and ultimate outcome remain open.

## 🛡️ Writing Guidance & Continuity References

* **Prose guidance**: The voice contract favors concrete language, distinct character registers, and physically grounded action. Editorial review also considers rhythm, clarity, and emotional effect.
* **Strict Demographics**: 4,200,000 total souls; 600,000 Ark berths across 6 hulls; 3,600,000 condemned to the sub-tiers.
* **Character Continuity Invariants**:
  * **Bram**: 13 winters, turning 14 on 30 October.
  * **Sanna**: Exactly 18 winters.
  * **Wulfila**: Died at 19 on the Tithe Gantry 40 days prior; surrendered 41 winters to the ledger.
  * **Varek Fairgunjis**: 8.0 feet (2.44 m), 300 years of service; 30 kg drop-forged Fault Maul (*FORTIS ET LIBER*).

## 🎬 Production Pipeline & Tooling

In addition to the literary work, this repository hosts the tooling, shot manifests, and audio suites for motion comic and cinematic animatic production:

### 1. Episode 001: *Eleven Bells* (`episodes/001_eleven_bells/`)
* **Audio Session**: REAPER production project ([`Eleven_Bells_Project.rpp`](episodes/001_eleven_bells/Eleven_Bells_Project.rpp)) with locked 1.000 playrate and natural vocal timbre.
* **Master Take 7 Dialogue**: 19 discrete vocal statements with 5.5s–8.0s theatrical breath pauses, DSP high-pass filtering, and sidechain ducking.
* **Production Specs**: [`EPISODE_SPEC.md`](episodes/001_eleven_bells/EPISODE_SPEC.md), [`EPISODE_MANIFEST.json`](episodes/001_eleven_bells/EPISODE_MANIFEST.json), and [`SHOT_LEDGER.csv`](episodes/001_eleven_bells/SHOT_LEDGER.csv).
* **Storyboard Plates**: 13 curated monochrome storyboards and Dark Horse / dieselpunk style panels.

### 2. Panel Studio (`tools/panel-studio/`)
* In-house node-based storyboard layout tool with lore boundary verification:
  * `lib/loreGuard.mjs`: Programmatic validation of narrative constraints and forbidden visual tropes.
  * `lib/assetBoundary.mjs`: Strict asset integrity enforcement.
  * `lib/badgeCompositor.mjs`: Automated stamping of chapter figure markers and record tags.
  * Automated testing: `npm test` (`__tests__/*.test.mjs`).

### 3. Animatic Exporter (`tools/export_animatic.py`)
* Python pipeline for compiling storyboard stills, timing cues, and broadcast audio stems into synchronized video animatics.

### 4. 3D Character Engineering & Kinematics (`tools/`, `blender/`)
* **Brothar Varek Fairgunjis**: 3D Blender asset pipeline from initial static silhouettes (`V2A.1`) through motion chassis rigging (`V2B`), salvage state (`V4.0`), and articulated multi-axis kinematics (`V50+`).
* Programmatic inspection tools: kinematic clearances, ground contact probes, and drop-forged Fault Maul socket analysis.
* Historical root-level builders, renderers, and DeepSeek helpers are organized under [`tools/legacy/`](tools/legacy/README.md). Blender MCP setup scripts are under [`tools/blender_setup/`](tools/blender_setup/README.md).
