## The Weight of a Lie

In the mountain city of Fairguni, nineteen-year-old Wulfila surrenders his life believing it will secure water for his family. Forty days later, his younger brother Bram recovers evidence that the North Cistern was full before Wulfila died.

Varek, an old man inside a three-tonne rescue machine, protects the workers opening a feeder. He carries their evidence to the official who can recall the approaching ram crews. The official already knows.

**Reading status:** revised reading draft. All twelve chapters are written; this does not claim a final edition or a completed production.

## The Thulans: Salvation

**Three months was the sentence. Five years later, they are still inside.**

Caught stealing bread from the upper Spire, Ansen and his sister Nessa are confined in steam-powered penal frames. The promised release never comes. When Varek frees Ansen, he goes looking for the person he cannot leave behind.

The siblings compete, bicker, and find humour in hard working lives. Under pressure, the teasing gives way to private care. Their reunion offers a future worth trying to reach.

**Canon status:** Salvation is the canonical companion story. Eleven Bells is the earlier pre-story prototype.

**Production state:** working graphic-novel pilot revision 3, with 16 story pages and 66 panels. The newer relationship and pacing direction has not yet been rewritten into that pilot. Final art, mechanical blocking, music, and the search route remain in development. The gallery and handler scenes belong to Salvation canon; their final blocking remains in development. They have not been inserted into the novella manuscript.

## The rebellion novellas

The planned continuation follows a rebellion rising through the Mountain toward the Spire. Each community faces its own problem: refugees needing shelter, loyalists protecting what they have, workers who cannot afford to miss a shift, and officials changing their public language as allegiances shift.

Reports of what happened below reach the next district before the rebellion does. Those receiving the reports already have households, obligations, and reasons to fear what comes next. Their choices change the rebellion and the lives depending on the same water, air, food, and transport.

The state's monument appropriates Varek's defiance; Bram's invitation to read the preserved order together supplies a small organizational seed. Varek remains dead. Volume count, titles, floor numbering, sequel dates, and ultimate outcome remain open.
